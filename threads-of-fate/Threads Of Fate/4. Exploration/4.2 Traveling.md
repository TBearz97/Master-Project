Traveling is an important part of any adventure, it's filled with monsters, wonders and sometimes even friendly faces. There are two main aspects of travel that requires attention from the party. Navigation and scouting.
 
# **Navigation**  
Navigation is the act of keeping track of where you are going and making sure you are always taking the optimal paths. When traveling you should assign at least one player character to navigation. Each day the navigator needs to make a [[TODO]] test with Difficulty varying based on the environment you are traversing. Weather and other effects can increase the difficulty. On a success you progress number of hexes based on which mounts you use. There are various ways of traveling and they affect the rate of travel as well as the safety of your journey. They are as following:

| **Means of Travel** | **Rate of Travel** | **Safety** |
| ------------------- | ------------------ | ---------- |
| On Foot             | 1                  | 0          |
| On Horseback        | 4                  | 1          |
| With Caravan        | 2                  | 2          |
| Armed Escort        | 2                  | 3          |

More about acquiring mounts, vehicles and escorts in the Equipment Section.
 
Should you fail the test, roll 1d6 and move the direction associated with the dice.

- 1: North
- 2: North East
- 3: South East
- 4: South
- 5: South West
- 6: North West

Also for each time you fail a Navigation test, increase the difficulty by 1, until you have found a landmark, road sign or city.
 
# **Scouting**

![Exported image](Exported%20image%2020241105151642-0.png)
Travel is handled by looking at the map above. There are different ways of traveling, some are faster, some are slower, and some have more danger and excitement. The different ways of travel and their risks are as follows:

- Walking on foot on a path, Event 2, Travel 1
- Horses on a path, Event 2, Travel 4
- Caravan on a path, Event 1, Travel 2
- Walking on foot no path, Event 3, Travel 1/2
- Horses no path, Event 3, Travel 2
- Caravan no path, Event 2, Travel 1
 
Event refers to the risk of an event happening during a day, event can be anything from a pack of wolfs attacking, to finding a mysterious pyramid. Any event gives the players something to do, explore and survive during travel. The GM rolls a number of times equal to the Event number given for the travel type the party chooses.

Travel refers to the number of hexagons you are traveling during a day, however this can be affected by terrain, events and choices.
