The bestiary is a descriptive treatise on various animals and entities found all around the world. A place where information about their location, habit and other various trivia gets stored during play, the bestiary also includes meta game information about their stats (health, defenses, etc.) which lets you learn how to deal with certain enemies better. 
# **How to progress the bestiary**
The main way of progressing the bestiary is by encountering the specific beast or creature. After having dealt with them, via combat, stealth or in rare cases even dialogue, you are allowed to progress the bestiary by performing a [[TODO]] test. On a successful test you get obtain one piece of information per creature dealt with. You can spend 1 IP to gain 1 additional piece of information.
