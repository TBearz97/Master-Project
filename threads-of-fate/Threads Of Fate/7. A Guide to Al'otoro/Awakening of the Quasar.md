When the Quasar awakened the societies that had lived in the dark perished. Only a few lucky survivors that we're already in shelter survived, and they we're force to start a new life underground.
 
During the next 5000 years, the societies of the dark with the help of The Luminescent started rebuilding and exploring the World Below. New life has started appearing on the surface, life that thrives in the shining light of the Quasar. Some elves also started making a return to the surface, while the dwarves disappeared.
 
Then for the next 2000 years kingdom's and cities rose and fell. Dragon's roamed around freely killing and eating whatever they desired. War and chaos dominated the lands of Al'otoro until the establishment of the Orotari Kingdom and the First Era began.
