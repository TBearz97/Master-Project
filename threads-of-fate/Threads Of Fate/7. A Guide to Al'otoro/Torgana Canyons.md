Home of the Goliaths, these large people are a tribal society led by the strongest. The entire tribe is known to be great warriors and strategically better at combat than most other races. However, due to their method of choosing a leader the tribes rarely work together, and when they do it only lasts as long as the current leader lives. Challenges of leader ship happens often, a Chieftain rarely dies of old age or steps down from their position and their leadership on average lasts 6 months.
