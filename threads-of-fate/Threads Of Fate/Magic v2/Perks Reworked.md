**Master Sorcerer**  
**Requirements:** Arcana Tag, Skilled Sorcerer, Level 10  
Ranks: 2  
If you have cast a spell with at least one variety, reduce the difficulty by Ranks in this Perk. When selecting this Perk increase the level requirement by 5.
 
**Novice Sorcerer**  
**Requirements:** Arcana Rank 2  
**Ranks:** 3  
When casting spells, for each Rank in this Perk reduce the price for buying the corresponding dice by 1.
 
**Skilled Sorcerer**  
**Requirements:** Arcana Rank 3, Level 5  
You can now benefit from variety casting.
 
**Creative Magic**  
**Requirements:** Arcana Rank 4  
For 2 IP, you can add any Effect or Quality to the damage of a spell.
 
**Twinned Sorcery**  
**Requirements:** Arcana Rank 4  
When casting a spell, you can add a Variety from another spell to that cast.
 
**Magic Trap**  
**Requirement:** Arcana Rank 5  
When casting any spell in any form, you can spend 2 IP to store it as a trap somewhere in the environment rather than unleash it. When you do so, the Difficulty is 1 and increases by 1 for each extra IP spent. Detecting requires a PER + Investigation, Disarming requires INT + Arcane.
 
**Defiance**  
**Requirement:** Discipline Tag, Level 4  
**Ranks:** 4  
Through great discipline you are able to simply defy the effects of magic. For each rank in this perk increase your magical DR by 1. This perk has no effect if you are wearing armor. Each time you take this perk increase the level requirement by 2.
 
**Debater**  
**Requirement:** Discipline Rank 2  
In a calm and collected manner you seem to be able to present facts in a persuasive way. Whenever you are trying to persuade someone to change their mind you can use Discipline rather than Speech for that test if you desire.
 
**Mind Fortress**  
**Requirement:** Discipline Rank 3  
Your mind is a difficult thing to gain access to. Whenever you succeed a struggle using the Discipline skill, each additional success also generate complications for the opponent.
 
**Clarity**  
**Requirement:** Discipline Rank 4  
After a successful Focus Minor Action, reduce the difficulty of Variety casts by 1 (minimum of 1).
 
**Lasting Focus**  
**Requirement:** RES 7  
**Ranks:** 2  
Whenever you succeed on a Focus Minor Action, you can choose to keep it for rounds equal to Ranks in this perk.
 
**Great Focus**  
**Requirement:** RES 8, Level 5  
Each success on a Focus Minor Action, gain an additional success.
 
**Sharp Mind**  
**Requirement:** RES 6, Level 4  
**Ranks:** 2  
When you are forced to make a Concentration test in order to maintain Concentration, reduce the difficulty by 1 for each Rank in this perk (minimum 0). For each rank of this perk, increase the level requirement by 2
 
**Master Sorcerer**  
**Requirements:** Arcana Tag, Skilled Sorcerer, Level 8  
**Ranks:** 3  
Rank 1: If you have cast a spell with at least one variety, reduce the difficulty by 1.  
Rank 2: If you have cast a spell with at least two varieties, reduce the difficulty by 2.  
Rank 3: If you have cast a spell with at least three varieties, reduce the difficulty by 3.  
Increase the level requirement by 4 for each time you take this Perk.
 
**Novice Sorcerer**  
**Requirements:** Arcana Rank 2, Level 1  
**Ranks:** 3  
Rank 1: Reduce the price of the first additional d20 by 1.  
Rank 2: Reduce the price of the second additional d20 by 1.  
Rank 3: Reduce the price of the third additional d20 by 1.  
Increase the level requirement by 2 for each time you take this Perk.
 
**Skilled Sorcerer**  
**Requirements:** Arcana Rank 3, Level 5  
You can now benefit from variety casting.
 
**Quiet Casting**  
**Requirements:** Arcana Rank 3, Stealth Rank 3, Skilled Sorcerer  
Unlock a new Variety option called Quiet Casting.  
(+ Difficulty 1) Quiet Casting: The spell now benefits from the sneak attack rules.

**Master of Chaos**  
**Requirement:** Arcana Rank 6  
After a successful spell cast, you can spend 1 Luck to add/remove one odd numbered option from the Magic Complication Table.
