**Imbue Elements**  
**Keywords:** Consentration  
**Difficulty:** 1  
**Base Cast:**  
You summon the power of the elements to imbue a weapon. Imbued weapons can use the Weapons Attribute + Arcana for the attack.

- Earth: Rock covers the weapon giving it the Stun effect.
- Air: Wind surrounds your weapon giving it the Light Quality.
- Water: Cold water crystalizes around the weapon giving it the Freeze Quality.
- Fire: Fire engulfs your weapon giving it the Fire effect.

**Variety Cast:**

- (+ Difficulty 2) Additional Element: Imbue the weapon with an additional element.
- (+ Difficulty X) Conjure Weapon: Conjure a weapon with the chosen element with the IP cost being the rarity of the chosen weapon.

**IP Spend:**

- 2 IP: The spell binds to the weapon until the end of the scene, you no longer require consentration to keep it.

**Conjure a storm**  
**Keywords:** Consentration  
**Difficulty:** 1  
**Base Cast:**  
You focus on the air around you and conjure a storm that obscure all vision through the zone giving a +1 Difficulty to all tests that require vision.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (+ Difficulty 1) Blizzard: The zone becomes Difficult Terrain 1.
- (+ Difficulty 2) Vortex: To exit the zone requires Difficulty 1 END + Athletics test.
- (+ Difficulty 2) Lightning: Lightning strikes down at creatures within the zone at the end of their turns. This deals 4 CD damage with the Stun Effect.

**IP Spend:**

- 2 IP: Increase Vision Penalty by 1.
- 2 IP: Increase Vortex Difficulty by 1.
- 2 IP: Increase Blizzard Terrain by 1.

**Nature's Balm**  
**Keywords:** Lasting  
**Difficulty:** Varies  
**Optimal Range:** Reach  
**Base Cast:**  
Conjure and apply a magical balm onto a nearby target. The balm can have the following effects:

- (+ Difficulty 2) Healing: The balm has a healing effect restoring 2 CD at the start of their turn, each time they roll at least one Effect it occurs the next turn as well.
- (+ Difficulty 1 + Target Defense) Toxins: The balm is poisonous to anyone except you, make an attack against a target dealing 4 CD with the Persistent(Poison) effect.

**Variety Cast:**

- (+ Difficulty 1) Curing: The balm has a curing effect, removing any poison, disease or persistent Effect the target is currently suffering from then ending the spell.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Sickening: Give the Toxins attack Infectious Effect.

**IP Spend:**

- 1-6 IP: Target an additional creature. The cost increases by 1 for each additional target up to 3.

**Hunter's Mark**  
**Keyword:** Consentration  
**Difficulty:** 1  
**Optimal Range:** M  
**Base Cast:**  
You mark an enemy to be hunted as your prey. This spell lasts 1 hour or until dispelled and with a range of 5km. The mark reduces the difficulty of Survival tests for tracking this enemy by 2.

- (+ Difficulty 1) Long Trail: Double the range of the spell.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Bleeding Mark: Add Persistent(Bleed) Effect to your damage against the target.

**Commune with the Wild**  
**Keywords:** Lasting  
**Difficulty:** 1

**Shapeshift**  
**Keywords:** Instant  
**Difficulty:** 1  
**Base Cast:**  
You can change your body into the form of a Normal animal. When casting this spell you gain all physical attributes of the animal(STR, AGI, END and PER) as well as all it's abilities, but keep your own max health. Any armor worn that does not contain metal transforms with you without any effect. Attacks made as a Beast uses their Melee Skill which is tagged. While shapeshifted you need to maintain consentration or revert back to your original form.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (+ Difficulty -1) Feral Attack: Transform only part of you into an animal, namely the part of the animal that allows for you to gain it's form of attack.
- (+ Difficulty 2) Greater Beast: Transform into a beast of Mighty type.
- (+ Difficulty 3) Become Legend: Transform into a beast of Legendary type.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Of Sky and Sea: Transform into a beast with swimming or flying.
- (+ Difficulty 1) It's Hide Thickens: Utilize any non-metal armor worn if it's better DR than the animal.

**IP Spend:**

- 2 IP: Ignore the first Injury inflicted while Shapeshifted.
- 2 IP: Automatically succeed the first consentration test.

**Exhale Fumes**  
**Keyword:** Instant, Lasting  
**Difficulty:** 1  
**Base Cast:**  
You unhinge your jaws and summon guttural fumes to cloud your current zone. Any one except you suffer from it's effects. Anyone inside the fumes suffer 4 CD damage.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (+ Difficulty 1) Toxic Fumes: The fumes is toxic to breath, the fumes gain the Persistent(Poison) Effect.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Necrotic Fumes: The fumes are completely rotten, the fumes gain the Necrotic Effect.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Thick Gas: The fumes form a thick gas, difficulty of tests relying on eyesight increase by 1 and difficulty of tests relying on smell increase by 2.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Disease Ridden: At the end of the targets turn they must succeed a END + Resistance test or roll on the disease table. If they get a disease it takes effect immediately.
- (+ Difficulty 2) Flammable: The fumes are flammable, should it come in contact with fire it will ignite and explode causing anyone inside the zone to take 8 CD with Fire and Knockdown Effect.

**Optimal Range:** C

**Control Water**  
**Keyword:** Consentration  
**Difficulty:** 1  
**Optimal Range:** C  
**Base Cast:**  
You gain the ability to control the flow of water. When you cast this spell you gain control of one square meters of free water. You can move this where you want but at a slow speed. The spell lasts as long as you can maintain concentration, each hour you need to make a concentration test with difficulty equal to previous concentration tests + 1. You can make new test with Variety options anytime during consentration, this test only has difficulty of the Variety option.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (+ Difficulty 1) Abundance of Water: Increase the amount of water by 2X.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Change Form: Turn the water liquid, gaseous or freezing.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Launch: Launch the water at a target for 4 CD with Knockdown Effect, if frozen add Freeze Effect, if gaseous add Necrotic Effect, and if add 2 CD for each square meter above the first.
