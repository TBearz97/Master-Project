- You do not benefit from the damage multiplier
- You do not benefit from the weapons Qualities or Effects
- You cannot use special attacks with the weapon

# **Ranged Weapons and Their Special Attacks**
Combat emphasizes versatility, tactical depth, and the unique qualities of each weapon type. This approach ensures that every weapon and attack serves a distinct role, rewarding players who master their equipment and combat strategies.

## **Special Attacks**
Special attacks allow melee weapons to transcend simple damage-dealing. Each weapon type has unique options that reflect its strengths and playstyle. These attacks balance risk and reward by modifying speed and adding effects, but requires targeting specific hit locations.

## **Sidearms**
Sidearms are compact, versatile ranged weapons designed for quick and decisive action in close-quarters combat. They excel in delivering high-impact shots with minimal preparation, making them ideal for emergencies, rapid responses, or as secondary weapons to complement heavier armaments. While limited in range and power compared to larger firearms, sidearms shine in their agility, ease of use, and versatility.

- **Blunderbuss**: A short-barreled firearm designed for close-quarters combat with a spread of projectiles.
- **Pistol**: A compact firearm for quick, precise shots at medium range.
- **Hand-Crossbow**: A compact crossbow that offers quiet, quick-firing capability with minimal setup.

## Marksman Weapons
Marksman Weapons are long-range, high-impact arms designed to deliver precise and devastating strikes. While slower to fire and often requiring reloading after each shot, these weapons excel at piercing armor, disrupting formations, and eliminating high-priority targets. Their power and precision make them the weapon of choice for sharpshooters and tacticians who dominate the battlefield with well-placed shots.

- **Musket**: A long-barreled firearm that delivers powerful, single-target damage at medium to long range.
- **Crossbow**: A mechanical weapon that fires bolts with precision and force, effective for silent and deadly ranged attacks.
- **Arbalest**: A heavy crossbow that sacrifices speed for unmatched power and armor-piercing capabilities.

## **Bows**
Bows are versatile and efficient ranged weapons, favored for their balance of speed, precision, and adaptability. They excel in situations requiring rapid fire and mobility, making them ideal for hunters, scouts, and warriors who value accuracy and agility. With the ability to deliver both single-target precision and area-effect attacks, bows offer a wide range of tactical options.

- **Short Bow**: A lightweight and compact bow designed for quick shots at short to medium range.
- **Hunting Bow**: A standard bow offering a balance of speed and power, ideal for general-purpose use.
- **War Bow:** A reinforced bow built for combat, offering superior power at the cost of speed.
- **Long Bow**: A large, powerful bow with exceptional range and force, favored for its battlefield dominance.

# **Ranged Weapons Complications**  
The following are complications that might occur while wielding a ranged weapon. Roll a CD for one of the complications.

- **(1) Fumbled:** When reaching for your ammunition, you fumble and loose it, making the next attack with that weapon have an additional speed of +15. 
- **(2) Overextended:** Your attack left you unable to effectively defend yourself; reduce your defenses by 2 until the start of your next turn.
- **(Blank) Wear and Tear:** The weapon has seen better days, and no longer functions as well as it once did. Reduce the weapon’s reliability. If it was unreliable and you were forced to reduce it, the weapon breaks beyond repair.
- **(Effect) Snap(Bows Only):** The bow string snaps. You need to use the Interact Minor Action on the weapon before you can use it again.
- **(Effect) Click…(Crossbows and Firearms only):** You pull the trigger again, but nothing happens. You need to use the Interact minor action on the weapon before you can fire it again.
