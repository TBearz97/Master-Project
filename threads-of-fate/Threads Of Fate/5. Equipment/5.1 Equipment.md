Everyone wants more gear. The instinct to hoard is a common one among the people dwelling in the world, and for good reason: you never know when something will be the difference between life and death. Gold are useful but selling your last few arrows for a fistful of gold does you no good if you get into a fight before you can spend them.

# **Gold and Currency**  
Al'otoro boast a bewildering range of currencies: coinage of different metals, alloys, weights, and denominations, ranging from copper, bronze, silver, and gold, stamped with the likenesses of royalty or scratched with rough glyphs, even hacked into smaller parts and spent piecemeal. Keeping track of all of this currency is hardly the stuff of high adventure, and in Threads of Fate the default unit of currency your character will deal with is Gold. Generally, your characters will have to spend Gold on minor things like room and board, travel short distances, or the casual expenses that occur day-to-day.

# **Finding and Purchasing Items**  
Most common items are available for purchase at any settlement. However, obtaining something new or more significant is one goal of acquiring wealth. These acquisitions take more time and more effort to obtain, often requiring an item be crafted to individual specifications, or requiring heated haggling and negotiation to get to an acceptable final price. It may take hours or days to find the right trader, and if an item is to be made specially or customized to your character’s specifications, it may take days or even weeks more to complete. In order to actually find a new or different item outside your character’s current range of gear, you must spend time [[3.2 Downtime - Incomplete#**Browsing **|Browsing]], then the gamemaster must set a Difficulty for the item, represented by a variety of factors, availability in the area, rarity, quality, relative expense, nature, legality (or illegality), even current events in the area such as war or famine. Similarly, arms and armor might be easier to find in war-ravaged areas, due to battlefield scavenging or soldiers mustering out and selling their gear. The Difficulty can also be modified by other factors, particularly Foreign Language and Social Factors. Your character must make a skill test determined by the GM. For example if the item is legal, you may need to attempt a Speech test to convince a reluctant trader to sell the item. Or, if the item is illegal you might need to attempt a Stealth test to avoid attention during your search. Once the item has been located, your character should pay for it (if not, see Gaining Items by Theft or Violence, below). Each item has a distinct cost, reflected in the amount of Gold needed to afford the item. This price is modified by your character’s Renown [[TODO]], which may reduce the cost of the item to 0. In these cases, your character’s reputation is so great that the trader or artisan may wish to give your character the item as a gift, to curry favor or gain your patronage. Generally, your gamemaster should limit the number of these item acquisition rolls to one per scene or session, and failure with one increases the Difficulty should it be attempted again.
