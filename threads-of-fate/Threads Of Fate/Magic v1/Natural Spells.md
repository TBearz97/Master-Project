**Imbue Elements**  
**Keywords:** Consentration, Novice
 
You summon the power of the elements to imbue a weapon. The effect an difficulty differs between the elements.

- 2 IP Earth: Rock covers the weapon giving it the Stun effect.
- 2 IP Air: Wind surrounds your weapon giving it the Light Quality.
- 3 IP Water: Cold water crystalizes around the weapon giving it the Freeze Quality.
- 4 IP Fire: Fire engulfs your weapon giving it an extra 2 CD and the Fire effect.  
The spell can also be cast with the following effects:

- 3 IP Bind the Elements: The spell binds to the weapon until the end of the scene, you no longer require consentration to keep it.
- X IP Conjure Weapon: Conjure a weapon with the chosen element with the AP cost being the rarity of the chosen weapon.

**Conjure a storm**  
**Keywords:** Consentration, Novice
 
You focus on the air around you and conjure a storm with one or more of the following traits:

- 3 IP Blizzard: Turn an zone into difficult terrain and give all attacks landing within the zone the Freezing Quality.
- 2X IP Vortex: To exit the zone requires Difficulty X Endurance + Athletics test.
- 1 IP Fog: Obscure all vision through the zone.
- 3X IP Lightning: Lightning strikes down at X targets at the start of your turns. This deals 3 CD damage with the Stun Effect.
 
**Nature's Balm**  
**Keywords:** Lasting, Skilled
 
Conjure and apply a magical balm onto a nearby target. The balm can have the following effects:

- 5 IP Healing: The balm has a healing effect restoring 1 CD at the start of every turn.
- 4 IP Curing: The balm has a curing effect, removing any poison, disease or persistent Effect the target is currently suffering from then ending the spell.
- 3X IP Additional targets: Apply the balm to X additional targets. If you are not within reach of a target you want to apply the balm to, you can save the additional balm to be applied during the next round no action required.
- 2X IP Toxins: The balm is poisonous to anyone except you, make an attack against a target dealing X CD with the Persistent(Poison) effect.
- 5 IP Sickening: Target makes a END + Resistance test, if they fail the target must roll on the disease table and the disease takes effect immediately .

**Hunter's Mark**  
**Keyword:** Consentration, Novice
 
You mark an enemy to be hunted as your prey. Unless otherwise specified this spell lasts 1 hour or until dispelled and with a range of 5km. The mark can have the following effects:

- 2 IP Tracking Mark: Any reduce the difficulty of Survival tests for tracking this enemy by 2.
- 2 IP Strong Will: Double the duration of the spell X times.
- 2 IP Long Trail: Double the range of the spell X times.
- 3 IP Bleeding Mark: Add Persistent(Bleed) Effect to your damage against the target.  
**Control Plants**  
**Keywords:** Consentration, Skilled
 
You gain control over nearby plants causing decay, overgrowth and other various effects to aid you. The spell can have the following effects:

- 4 IP Entangle: Cause plants to grow and entangle a creature, dealing 3 CD with Grappling and Spread Effects.
- 5 IP Wall of Thorns: Have thorns grow in the shape of the wall, anyone within reach takes 3 CD upon entering reach and starting their turn in reach.
- 2 IP Decaying Touch: Anyone who comes in contact with plants you control takes 2 CD Necrotic damage.
- 2 IP Poisonous Touch: Anyone who comes in contact with plants you control takes 2 CD Persistent(Poison) damage.

**Commune with the Wild**  
**Keywords:** Lasting, Novice
 
You reach out for the nearest animal to appear. The animal is of Normal type. You can ask it for assistance with knowledge, fighting or surveillance. Unless otherwise stated, the animal will appear, fulfill the request to the best of ability and leave as soon as possible. The spell works with the following effects:

- 2 IP More Ferocious: Change the animal summoned to one of Mighty type.
- 4 IP Legendary Beast: Change the animal summoned to one of Legendary type.
- 1 IP Wisdom of the Wild: Ask a question and have it answered truthfully to the best of its knowledge.
- 2 IP Sanctity of the Pack: Summon X additional animals.
- 3 IP Assistance of the Wild: Ask a task and have it done to the best of its ability.

**Shapeshift**  
**Keywords:** Novice
 
You can change your body into the form of a Normal animal. When casting this spell you gain all physical attributes of the animal(STR, AGI, END and PER) as well as all it's abilities, but keep your own max health. Any armor worn that does not contain metal transforms with you without any effect. Attacks made as a Beast uses the Unarmed Skill. While shapeshifted you ignore the first injury inflicted on you, but the second takes effect and reverts you back to your normal form. The spell can take the following effects:

- 1 IP Feral Attack: Transform only part of you into an animal, namely the part of the animal that allows for you to gain it's form of attack.
- 3 IP Transform: Transform into a beast of Normal type.
- 5 IP Greater Beast: Transform into a beast of Mighty type.
- 7 IP Legendary Transformation: Transform into a beast of Legendary type.
- 2 IP Of Sky and Sea: Transform into a beast with swimming or flying.
- 3 IP It's Hide Thickens: Utilize any armor worn if it's better DR than the animal. Any metal armor transforms with you, but only for half effect.

**Hunter's Shot**  
**Keyword:** Novice
 
Empowers your ammunition to have additional Effects and Qualities. Any attack made with a ranged weapon now has been empowered. The empowerment can have the following effects:

- X IP Fire Shot: The next X shots have the Fire Effect.
- X IP Frost Shot: The next X shots have the Frost Quality.
- X IP Toxic Shot: The next X shots have the Persistent(Poison) Effect.
- X IP Necrotic Shot: The next X shots have the Necrotic Effect.
- X IP Revealing Shot: The next X shots have the Revealing Effect.
- 2X IP Fast Shot: The next X attacks have double fire rate. If fire rate was 0 it increases to 1.

**Exhale Fumes**  
**Keyword:** Skilled
 
You unhinge your jaws and summon guttural fumes to cloud your current zone. Any one except you suffer from it's effects. Anyone inside the fumes suffer 4 CD damage. The fumes can have the following effects:

- 5 IP Toxic Fumes: The fumes is toxic to breath, the fumes gain the Persistent(Poison) Effect.
- 5 IP Necrotic Fumes: The fumes are completely rotten, the fumes gain the Necrotic Effect.
- 2 IP Thick Gas: The fumes form a thick gas, difficulty of tests relying on eyesight increase by 1 and difficulty of tests relying on smell increase by 2.
- 7 IP Flammable: The fumes are flammable, should it come in contact with fire it will ignite and explode causing anyone inside the zone to take 8 CD with Fire and Knockdown Effect.
- 5 IP Disease Ridden: At the end of the targets turn they must succeed a END + Resistance test or roll on the disease table. If they get a disease it takes effect immediately.

**Shape the Earth**  
**Keyword:** Novice
 
You focus your will to shape the earth to your desire. Shape the Earth can have the following effects:

- 1 IP Sand Toss: Hurl a sand from the ground at target enemy dealing 3 CD with Stun effect. Creatures with eye protection or without eyes are immune to the Stun effect of this ability.
- 3 IP Rock Toss: Hurl a rock from the ground at target enemy dealing 5 CD with Burst, Knockdown and Vicious Effect.
- 3 IP Shape the Land: Choose a zone and make the terrain more or less difficult. Difficulty X.
- 4 IP Moving Plates: Move a plate of earth beneath a creature anywhere within an adjacent zone.

**Control Water**  
**Keyword:** Novice
 
You gain the ability to control the flow of water. When you cast this spell you gain control of 2 square meters of free water. The spell lasts as long as you can maintain concentration, each hour you need to make a conversation test with difficulty equal to previous concentration tests + 1. During concentration you can add additional effects. The spell can have the following effects:

- 2 IP Abundance of Water: Increase the amount of water by 2X.
- 2 IP Change Form: Turn the water liquid, gaseous or freezing.
- 2 IP Launch: Launch the water at a target for 4 CD with Knockdown Effect, if frozen add freeze effect, if gaseous add necrotic effect, and if more than 2 square meters add 2 CD.
- 3 IP Control Water: Gain control over the water and move it around.
