**Aura of the gods**  
**Keywords:** Consentration, Oathsworn, Skilled
 
Create an aura that follows you and covers your current zone and creates a magical effect based on your oathsworn God.

- Aura of the Quasar: Roll 4 CD. Divide the result between each other creature inside your zone. If you heal less than amount of creatures choose who to heal.
- Aura of the Soulless: Any creatures within the aura that has three or more injuries instantly dies.
- Aura of the Luminescent: Any creatures at any time within this aura is revealed.
- Aura of the Obscured: Name an object and every instance of said object is hidden within the aura.
- Aura of the Ferocious: Any creature with the beast tag will see you as an ally.
- Aura of the Erudite: Reduce the difficulty of Intelligence tests by 1.
- Aura of the Artisan: All weapons within the aura gains the reliable Quality. Creatures within the aura can also ignore one complication on Craft tests.
- Aura of the Vengeful: If an creature within the aura was damaged during this turn, their next attack gains the Vicious Effect.
- Aura of the Sentinel: All creatures within this aura has reduced move speed away from the center of the aura(if the aura is centered on you or someone else, that person is not affected). Any creature that takes the Move minor action gets pulled into reach of the center of the aura. Any creature that takes the Sprint Action can only reach the adjacent zone at the edge.
- Aura of the Fiery: All weapons within the aura gains the Fire Effect.
- Aura of the Hearth of the Sand: Cause or dampen a sand storm as a Free Action once on subsequent turns.
 
You can cast Aura of the Gods in different ways with one the following options:

- 5 IP Center on Self: Cast an Aura that centers on yourself.
- 6 IP Center on Other: Cast an Aura that covers a specific zone or centers on another creature.
 
If you cast this spell using only the Aid of Another option, the location of the Aura will be determined by the GM based on the Gods invoked.

**Faithful Light**  
**Keyword:** Instant, Novice
 
Through prayer you shine a light that emits from you in a straight line towards the target. The light can have the following effects:

- 3 IP Healing: When the light reaches another creature it regains 2 CD HP.
- 5 IP Close wounds: When the light reaches another creature it can remove one injury.
- 4 IP Cure corruption: When the light reaches another creature you can heal necrotic damage. This only removes the necrotic damage and does not increase max HP or current HP.

**Divine Smite**  
**Keyword:** Instant, Novice
 
Brandish your weapon with divine power. The attack deals the weapons normal damage and effects plus one or more of the following options:

- 3 IP Vicious Smite: The attack deals an extra 2 CD and gets the Vicious 1 effect.
- 3 IP Forceful Smite: The attack pushes the target back into the adjacent zone. If the adjacent zone is blocked the target and whatever is blocking it takes 2 CD damage.
- 2 IP Necrotic Smite: The attack gains the necrotic effect.
- 2 IP Brandishing Smite: The attack leaves an imprint on the target limb that cannot be removed. The imprint takes form of the symbol of your god, any followers of your god can sense when an imprint is within the same scene. The only thing that can remove this imprint is if the target is forgiven by the chosen god.

**Blessing**  
**Keyword:** Consentration, Novice
 
Your pray for another creature giving it benefits and increased chance of success. The blessing spell can be cast in the following ways:

- 2X IP Divine Skill: You increase one skill of another creature by X amount. This can exceed the normal limit of 6, but no higher that 8.
- 1 IP Common Blessing: You pray to a god and asks them to watch over a person, homestead or something else. Should your request align with your gods interests then the recipient's general luck improves at DM's discretion.

_Example: A priest blesses his family's homestead before leaving for adventure. During the adventure his village is raided, but through his gods blessing his family survived the attack._

- 4 IP Divine Attribute: You chose a creature and a attribute, the creature then has 1 reroll on all tests with chosen attribute.
- 4 IP Divine Inspiration: Increase the Party's AP pool by 2.

**Word of Power**  
**Keyword:** Master
 
Speak a Word of Power that legends say descended from Gods. The words have various effects and you can speak several with one cast. The Words are the following:

- 10 IP Kill: You focus on another creature as you speak this word. If the target creature has two or more injuries it dies.
- 8 IP Heal: You focus on another creature as you speak this word, the target creature restores all injuries suffered.
- 9 IP Pain: You focus on another creature as you speak this word, you deal 8 CD damage with the Intense Quality.
- 10 IP Hope: You utter the word quietly for yourself. You increase the Party's AP pool by 2 and fills it at the start of each of your turns.

**Watchful Presence**  
**Keyword:** Consentration, Skilled
 
This spell blesses you when you fall below certain HP threshold. This happens after an attack has completed and won't affect unconscious or dead creatures. The spell can have the following effects:

- 5 IP Sentinels Retaliation: When you fall below 50% HP, make an attack targeting the most recent attacker within reach.
- 5 IP Favor from the Soulless: When you reach 0 HP, go back to 10% HP.
- 6 IP Invigorating Light: When you fall below 25% HP, heal 4 CD for every 5 levels.
- 7 IP Artisan's Protection: When you fall below 75% HP, gain 2 DR to all damage types for the next 3 rounds.
- 7 IP Become Obscured: The next time you take damage become invisible for 3 rounds or until you interact with another creature.
- 5 IP Surrounding Sands: When you fall below 10% HP, a sand wall emerges from the ground in a direction of your choosing. The wall is 5 meters high, 5 meters long and 1 meter thick. While behind this sand wall you gain Cover 3.

**Prayer of Protection**  
**Keyword:** Consentration, Novice
 
You pray for a Gods protection, this affect 2 creatures of your choice. This spell can have the following effects:

- 4 IP Safety from the Necrotic: Increase necrotic DR by 2.
- 3 IP Anti-Magic Field: Increase magic DR by 2.
- 3 IP Resilience: Increase physical DR by 2.
- 2 IP Determination: Ignore the first Effect against you each round.

**Restoration**  
**Keyword:** Consentration, Skilled
 
Restore vital essence to a creature so it may live again. The spell has a difficulty of 5 and revives a creature to life, for the creature to remain alive the creature must heal all injuries and the caster must maintain concentration for the next 24 hours or the creature will die again. The spell can have the following effects:

- 2 IP Complete the spell: The spell no longer requires concentration.
- 3X IP True Restoration: The spell also heals X injuries.
- 2 IP Request more time: The spell last for an additional 24 hours.

**Call for Aid**  
**Keyword:** Lasting, Oathsworn, Novice
 
You ask your oathsworn God for aid and summon minions from their domain to aid you. The spell lasts until the end of the scene. Minions and their associated God:

- The Obscured: Eldritch Beings. Acts in combat on it's own unless commanded.
- The Fiery: Flame Elementals. Acts in combat on it's own unless commanded.
- The Ferocious: Spirit Beasts. Acts in combat on it's own unless commanded.
- The Vengeful: Sinful Beings. Acts in combat on it's own unless commanded.
- The Quasar: Virtuous beings. Acts in combat on it's own unless commanded.
- The Soulless: Undead beings. Acts in combat on it's own unless commanded.
- The Luminescent: Crystal Elementals. Acts in combat on it's own unless commanded.
- The Hearth of the Sand: Sand Elementals. Acts in combat on it's own unless commanded.
- The Erudite: Scholars to Aid you with Lore tests. Will roll assistance on your tests, DM controls these.
- The Artisan: Henchmen to Aid you with Craft tests. Will roll assistance on your tests, DM controls these.
- The Sentinel: Shield Bearers to Aid you with Parry tests. Will roll assistance on your tests, DM controls these.

The spell can have the following additional effects:

- 2 IP Reinforcements: X Normal creatures.
- 2 IP Competence: X Creatures becomes Mighty instead of Normal.
- 4 IP Of Legends: X Creatures becomes Legendary instead of Normal.

**Avatar of the Gods**  
**Keywords:** Consentration, Oathsworn, Master
 
Become the embodiment of your oathsworn God by making a cast of AP/Difficulty 9. This Powerful spell increases you Max Health equal to you current level, it increases damage of all your weapon attacks by 4 CD. AP cost of Religion spells reduced by 2. Immunity to Necrotic and Increase to Physical and Magical DR by 3. The spell lasts for 3 rounds, but increase by 2 for each enemy killed. The spell can have the following additional effects:

- 2 IP Lasting empowerment: The spell lasts for 3 additional rounds.
- 3 IP True Faith: No longer requires concentration.
