**Choosing your Homeland and Roles**
 
Homeland determines what roles your character might have had before they ended up with a life of adventure. Was your character a solider for the Orotari Kingdom, a trader from Quasarian Empire, an Elder from the Ur'Holdum Jungle or maybe a Dragon Knight. This section lets you get a better idea who your character is as well as determines starting equipment, language and base knowledge about the world it also unlocks specific Perks. Any weapons gained from this section you also gain proficiency with. Referencing your background in a organic matter should be rewarded with a Luck point, however this is at the GMs discretion.

**Character Creation Summary**
 
When making a character you will be going through a few steps in order to determine their attributes, skills, backstory, perks and equipment. Every character starts at level 1, unless your GM says otherwise.  
The steps to creating a character are as follows:

- Choosing your Origin
- Choosing your Homeland
- Distributing Attributes
- Distributing Skills
- Calculate your Derived Statistics
- Choosing your starting Perks
- Writing a backstory

**Distributing Attributes**
 
Every attribute starts with a rank of 5 (unless your origin in Step 1: Origin alters this). You have 5 points to spend to increase your attributes, and each point spent increases a single attribute by 1. Use these points to buy ranks in any attribute. No attribute may be increased above 10, although your Origin may alter this maximum for some attributes. You may reduce a attribute rank to 3, to gain points to spend on another attribute. Your attributes provide part of your target number for skill tests, and contribute to most of your derived statistics.
 
**Distributing Skills**
 
You have a total number of skill points equal to 9 plus your Intelligence, and each skill point may be spent to buy one rank in any skill. During character creation, you cannot increase any skill to a rank above 3, unless you’re creating a character of level 3 or above. Skills have a maximum rank of 6. You cannot increase a skill rank above 6 by any means.
 
After distributing skills you also get one Tag skill for free.
 
**Choosing your first Perks**
 
A character is more than the sum of their parts, and a character’s attributes and skills alone do not give a full picture of what they are truly capable of. Your character has an edge, called perks. Perks are the specific advantages of your personal approach to challenges. They take the form of a mechanical bonus—re-rolls, bonus Inspiration Points, or skill substitutions—that apply to a specific circumstance. Many perks have one or more requirements. These requirements must be met before you can select the perk for your character, like having a attribute at a minimum rank. Each perk has a condition and a benefit. The condition describes the circumstances in which the perk can be used, and the benefit describes what you can do with the perk.
 
You can choose 3 perks from the perks list that you meet the requirements for. Some perks have multiple ranks which can be selected—each rank counts as a single perk choice.
 
**Adding Base Equipment**
 
As our character sets out to explore the vast and wondrous lands of Al'otoro, they do not underestimate the importance of being prepared. With a keen eye for adventure and a heart full of courage, they gather the most essential and basic equipment needed to survive in this foreign terrain. **Writing Backstory**
 
Now that all the stats, and meta parts of the character is done, it's time to delve a little further into the role playing of it all. Using the choices you have made for your character write a short little story about your character. This should be anything from a short sweet summary to a full page of information. Writing a backstory is important for a roleplaying focused table, as it helps you, the GM and your fellow players get a better understanding of your character and it makes it easier for you to think of choices your character would make.
