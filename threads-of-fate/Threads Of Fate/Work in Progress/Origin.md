Human￼Humans are the result of cross breeding Pure Elves and Goliaths.
 
Goliath￼Appeared during the Awakening of the Quasar.
 
Pure Elf￼Evolved from Pale Elves during the Awakening of the Quasar.
 
Dwarf￼Evolved from Stone-Folk during the Awakening of the Quasar.
 
Halfling￼Halflings are the result of cross breeding Wild Elves and Dwarves.
 
Ashborne￼Ashborne evolved from Pure Elves after centuries of living in the Obsidian Desert.
 
Wild Elf￼Wild Elves evolved away from Pure Elves after rejecting their society and prefering living in tune with nature.
 
Orc￼Appeared during the Awakening of the Quasar.
 
Husk-Folk  
Husk-Folk evolved from Pure Elves after centuries of exposure to Necronium.
