Backgrounds describe your life before you began adventuring. Background provide you with two Skill Tags form the choices listed to further describe who you are and what you know, some also include equipment or Perks.

**Soldier**
 
**Description:**  
You have served with the Military and gained adequate military training as well as a proper education. After your service you get to keep your gear as reward for your service. However you are expected to report in at your nearest military post should get the call for duty.
 
**Skills:**  
Discipline, Command, Melee Weapons, Ranged Weapons, Lore or Warfare.
 
**Equipment:**  
Full set of Chain Mail or Leather Armor.

**Criminal**
 
**Description:**  
You have led a criminal life learning to talk you way out of sticky situations, sneaking in the dark and living life among the less honored.
 
**Skills:**  
Speech, Stealth, Investigation, Sleight of Hand or Insight.
 
**Equipment:**  
Bandits Outfit and Lockpick Set.
 
**Language:**  
Thieves' Cant.

**Spy**
 
**Description:**  
Employed by the Nation or a business, you've led a life of infiltration and intrigue, learning how to sneak around, read people and fit in.
 
**Skills:**  
Stealth, Speech, Insight, Investigation, Acrobatics or Society.
 
**Equipment:**  
An Outfit of your choice.

**Noble**
 
**Description:**  
You are part of a Nobel family or have spent a significant portion of your life becoming one. A Nobel life comes with great social standing, understanding of money and perhaps even leadership skills.
 
**Skills:**  
Discipline, Society, Command, Linguistics or Lore
 
**Equipment:**  
Nobels Attire and 100 Gold
 
**Perk:** Of the same Cloth  
Reduce Difficulty when Carousing with Nobles by 1.

**Farmer**
 
**Description:**  
You are a simple farmer, such a life teaches one how to interact with animals, living off the land, crafting you what you need and humble understanding of people.
 
**Skills:**  
Animal Handling, Insight, Survival, Craft or Athletics.
 
**Equipment:**  
A weapon of Rarity 0.
 
**Perk:** One of the People  
Reduce Society tests when dealing with commoners by 1.

**Sage**
 
**Description:**  
You are a kith of books and study. You possess a vast amount of knowledge on several subjects or perhaps specialized in a few.
 
**Skills:**  
Lore, Medicine, Arcana, Investigation or Navigation.
 
**Equipment:**  
Scribe's Robe
 
**Perk:** Educated  
Tag Skills increases a Skill by 3 rather than 2.

**Acolyte**
 
**Description:**  
You are a kith of faith. An acolytes life is spent learning about modesty and a deeper understanding of your religion.
 
**Skills:**  
Discipline, Arcana, Lore, Speech or Medicine.
 
**Equipment:**  
Priest's Garments.

**Entertainer**
 
**Description:**  
You are an entertainer traveling around, meeting people and enjoying the social aspects of life.
 
**Skills:**  
Speech, Society, Insight, Acrobatics, Parry or Linguistics.
 
**Equipment:**  
Performer's Outfit and an Instrument of Choice.

**Artisan**
 
**Description:**  
You are an artisan spending most of your life honing you craft. You might spend life becoming the best in one field or you might want to learn the basics of several, however other aspects of an artisans life also come with life lessons.
 
**Skills:**  
Society, Discipline, Sleight of Hand, Melee Weapons or Ranged Weapons.
 
**Equipment:**  
Outfit of your choice and Crafting Tools of your choice.
 
**Tag:** ￼Tag Craft.

**Mercenary**
 
**Description:**  
As a Mercenary you are familiar with combat, traveling and taking care of yourself.
 
**Skills:**  
Acrobatics, Melee Weapons, Ranged Weapons, Survival, Medicine, Parry or Resistance.
 
**Equipment:**  
Wanderer's Leather Clothing and a Weapon of Rarity 2 or lower.

**Pathfinder**
 
**Description:**  
As a Pathfinder you have spend your life helping others navigation through landscaped without roads.
 
**Skills:**  
Survival, Resistance, Athletics, Investigation or Stealth.
 
**Equipment:**  
Ranger's Outfit and a Map of your Homeland.
 
**Special:** ￼Tag Navigation.

**Trader**
 
**Description:**  
As a Trader you are familiar with the life on the road or sea as well as you are socially adept. You might have traveler across the border into neighboring nations.
 
**Skills:**  
Speech, Survival, Navigation or Insight.
 
**Special:** ￼Tag Society.
 
**Perk:** Haggle for a Living  
You can re-roll 1d20 when browsing, and always gain a 10% discount.

**Pirate**
 
**Description:**  
You have led a pirate's life learning to talk you way out of sticky situations, sailing on the open sea and living life among the less honored.
 
**Skills:**  
Speech, Stealth, Sleight of Hand, Insight or Navigation.
 
**Equipment:**  
Pirate's Attire.
 
**Perk:** Home at Sea  
You can re-roll 1d20 on Navigation tests while at sea. You also always gain Well-Rested when sleeping on a ship.

**Gladiator**
 
**Description:**  
As a Gladiator you are familiar with combat and taking care of yourself.
 
**Skills:**  
Warfare, Parry, Resistance or Athletics
 
**Equipment:**  
Tribal Outfit and a Weapon of Rarity 2 or lower.
 
**Special:**  
Tag Melee Weapons.

**Hunter**
 
**Description:**  
As a Hunter you are adept at tracking animals or the gathering of fruits and berries.
 
**Skills:**  
Animal Handling, Discipline, Ranged Weapons or Melee Weapons.
 
**Equipment:**  
Ranger's Outfit
 
**Special:**  
Tag Survival.

**Elder**
 
**Description:**  
As an Elder of the tribe you possess more knowledge than most and can offer wisdom to those who need it. Elder is merely a title and does not reflect age, one is named Elder when one shows an aptitude for learning and is proven to be wise.
 
**Skills:**  
Medicine, Lore, Survival, Animal Handling, Insight or Speech.
 
**Special:**  
Tag a free Skill.

**Beast Tamer**
 
**Description:**  
As a Beast Tamer you have had the responsibility of taming and keeping the animals of the tribe.
 
**Skills:**  
Survival, Athletics, Acrobatics, Medicine or Insight.
 
**Special:**  
Tag Animal Handling and gain an Animal Handling Perk.

**Dragon Knight**
 
**Description:**  
As a Dragon Knight you were brought up at one of the holds of the Dragon Knight Order. This could be because one or both your parents are a part of the order, because you lost your parents to a dragon attack at a young age or something else.
 
**Skills:**  
Melee Weapons, Ranged Weapons, Lore, Acrobatics, Parry, Warfare or Medicine.
 
**Equipment:**  
A set of Leather Armor or Scale Armor.
 
**Perk:** Trained to Kill  
Reduce the Difficulty of Survival tests when tracking Dragons by 1. Re-roll 1d20 on every damaging attack against a Dragon. After killing a Dragon local villages will celebrate you as a hero, this means you sleep, drink and eat for free for the next 3 days.

**Hermit**
 
**Description:**  
As a Hermit you have cast aside the stressful life among a society for the preferable life of solitude.
 
**Skills:**  
Survival, Lore, Arcana, Medicine or Discipline.
 
**Perk:** Secluded  
After character creation, gain a Perk in each of your Tag skills, but CHA tests suffer a permanent increase in difficulty by 2.
