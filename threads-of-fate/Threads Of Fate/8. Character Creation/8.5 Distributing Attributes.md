Every creatures capabilities are defined by six attributes. They describe the characters physical and mental abilities and determine what your character excels at. These attributes are Might, Agility and Endurance to describe the physical attributes and Intelligence, Charisma and Resolve for mental attributes. Each attribute can be ranked from 1-10, where 1 is very poor and 10 is very good. They can never be reduced below 1, but in special circumstances they can be increased above 10.
 
When creating a character every attribute starts with a rank of 5. You have 5 points to spend to increase your attributes, and each point spent increases a single attribute by 1. Use these points to buy ranks in any attribute. You may reduce an attribute rank to 1, to gain points to spend on another attribute.

**Endurance (END)**  
Your natural toughness and resilience influences how many health points you have, how easy you find it to shrug off the physical dangers of the world.
