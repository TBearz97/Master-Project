In the world of adventure and peril, every hero needs an edge. Inspiration Points and Luck serve as your reserves of inner strength and serendipity. These valuable resources can turn the tide of battle, unravel the most enigmatic puzzles, and steer you away from impending doom. By tapping into these reservoirs, you channel the essence of fortune and heroisms. Use them wisely, for they are the sparks that ignite the flames of your legend.
 
# **Inspiration Points**
 
Inspiration Points can be used in a variety of ways depending on situation. However they serve a quite difficult purpose depending on the situation. Following are the uses:
 
### **General:**
- **Buy rerolls (2 IP):** 
	When making any skill test, you can buy rerolls. When you do so you roll the dice again but must use the new result.
- **Skill Focus (X IP):** 
	When making any skill test, you can reduce the difficulty of the skill test to a minimum of 0. Each reduction costs 1 IP.
 
### **Combat:**
- **[[2.2 Damage, Effects and Qualities#**Damage Effects**|Effects]] (2 IP):** 
	After dealing damage with a weapon or spell you can spend IP to inflict the listed Effect onto the same target.
- **Inflict [[2.3 Injury and Healing#**Injuries**|Injury]] (3 IP):** 
	After dealing damage to a target, you may attempt to inflict and injury to a target. The target needs to make a Fortitude test, where half your damage dealt is applied as a penalty to their test.
- **Buy Action Points (X IP):** 
	Gain X action point this turn.
 
### **Exploration:**
- **Reduce Time (1 IP):** 
	Halve the time it takes to complete a task.
	
- **More Loot (1 IP):**
	Extra rolls on loot table.
	
- **Extra Bestiary Information (1 IP):**
	After a successful bestiary test, you can spend IP to learn additional information about the creature.

- **Reduce Materials Spent Crafting (1 IP):**

## **Saving Inspiration Points**  
If you don’t want to spend the Inspiration Points you generate immediately, you can save them for later. How many you can save is determined by Charisma and explained further in the character creation section later in this book. If your saved Inspiration Points exceeds you maximum Inspiration Point Pool, then you will lose any saved Inspiration Points that exceeds the maximum.
 
## **The Gamemaster's Inspiration Point Pool**  
Just like you, the GM has a pool of their own Inspiration Points to spend on their NPC’s actions. They start each quest with 1 IP per player. There is no maximum to how many Inspiration Points the GM can have in their pool.
   

# **Luck**
Luck provides Luck points that you can spend to shift the odds in your favor in a number of different ways. Each character starts their journey with 3 Luck Points each.  
You can spend Luck points to do one of the following options, both during freeform play or on your turn in combat.
 
- **Luck of the Draw**:
	You may spend a Luck point to introduce a helpful fact or detail about the situation you’re in, something that you’d have to be lucky to encounter. You could find just the right items or the right kind of ammo while you’re scavenging, find a key to a locked door, or a clue to a wizards puzzle. The gamemaster may prompt you with an opportunity to do this, or you may suggest when it could happen, but they can veto your suggestions, or ask you to spend multiple Luck points to make it happen.

 - **Stacked Deck:**
	Before you attempt a skill test, to gain a +5 to your Skill Rank.
	
- **Lucky Timing:**
	At any time during a combat encounter, decide exactly when each of your characters action takes place rather than letting Speed determine it.
 
 - **Miss Fortune:**
	Choose a target currently preforming a skill test and increase the difficulty of their test by 5.

 - **Lucky Find:**
	You may spend one or more Luck points to re-roll on loot tables. You may know each outcome before deciding if you want the original or new roll.
	
 - **I Know a Guy:**
	You may spend a Luck point to "know a guy" meaning that your character knows a person from the past that might prove helpful to the current situation.
 

## **Regaining Luck**  
Once you’ve spent a point, it can’t be used again until the gamemaster refreshes them, this could be when you reach a milestone in your quest, or when you start a new quest. When they are refreshed, you get all your Luck points back, no matter how many you’ve spent.
