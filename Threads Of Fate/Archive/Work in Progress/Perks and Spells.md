**Acrobatics**
 
**Agile**  
**Prerequisite:** Acrobatics Rank 2  
**Maximum Rank:** 3  
You may re-roll one d20 when attempting an Acrobatics test, but you must accept the new result. At Rank 2 you get two re-rolls. At Rank 3 you can choose which roll to use.
 
**Light Footed**  
**Prerequisite:** Agile  
**Maximum Rank:** 2  
Your acrobatic skills have taught you to be light on your feet. This enables you to move naturally through though terrain. For each rank of Light Footed, you can reduce the Difficulty of Acrobatic tests made to overcome difficulty terrain by 1.
 
**Float Like a Butterfly…**  
**Prerequisite:** Agile  
You move across the battle field with grace making you especially difficult to shoot. Whenever you take a action where you move during your turn, increase your Defense against ranged attacks by 1.
 
**Sting Like a Bee**  
**Prerequisite:** Float Like a Butterfly…, Acrobatics Tag  
You are hard to pin down and move around your targets. Whenever you take a action where you move, your next attack gains the Piercing 1 Effect.
 
**Feline Agility**  
**Prerequisite:** Agile Rank 2  
You have gain an extraordinary skill when it comes to jumping long distances. When making a Acrobatics test to jump or leap reduce the Difficulty by 1 (minimum 1). Should this reduce the Difficulty to 0, you won't need to make the test. Also you gain 2 CD cover when taking fall damage.
 
**Evasive**  
**Prerequisite:** Feline Agility, Acrobatics Tag  
You are adept enough in your acrobatics skill that you've realized it's easier to dodge attacks than parrying them. Whenever you were to take a Defend action, use Acrobatics to increase both Ranged and Melee Defense.  
**Animal Handling**  
**Friendly Presence**  
**Prerequisite:** Animal Handling Rank +1  
Animals seems to have an easier time trusting you than most due to a calm and friendly presence. On any Animal Handling test where you generate at least one success, you may immediately roll a number of bonus d20s equal to your ranks of Part of the Pack, up to the normal maximum of three bonus d20s. Any successes generated on these additional dice are added to the initial success total, Complications and AP on these additional dice may be ignored.  
**Faithful Companions**  
**Prerequisite:** Friendly Presence, Animal Handling Tag  
**Maximum Rank:** 2  
You recognize the needs of your animal companions and are rewarded with devotion. When you gain this perk, you must nominate a single animal you own. This becomes an animal companion. Animal Companions are 2 levels lower than you, Normal type and with the Beast keyword. Generally you can control your companion, though the gamemaster may guide its behavior in some instances. In combat it will act on it's own unless commanded. Whenever you succeed at a Survival test to find food, drink or shelter, you automatically find adequate food and shelter for your animal companion(s) as well, without needing to spend AP or increase the difficulty of the task. You may gain an additional rank in this perk at level 4 for an additional companion.  
**Strong Companions**  
**Prerequisite:** Faithful Companions, Animal Handling Rank 4+, Level 5  
**Maximum Rank:** 4  
Your devotion to animal training has paid off. With adequate training and commitment your companions are now stronger. Choose one of your animal companions to gain one of the following:

- Gain one more level
- Increase Type by one step

Each time you take this perk increase the level requirement by 5  
**Animal Instinct**  
**Prerequisite:** Friendly Presence  
You have come to recognize when animals are reacting to the environment. Any time you are in the company of animals and need to make an Investigation test, you may substitute the Animal Handling skill instead if desired.  
**One with the Beasts**  
**Prerequisite:** Animal Instinct, Animal Handling Rank 3  
Wild or tamed matters not to you: all animals are kin and to be afforded due respect. Any time you attempt to direct an animal to take an action that goes against its instinct or training (including instances when the animal is in service of another) you can pay 1 AP to reduce the Difficulty of the test by one step. Additionally, if AP is spent to obtain information using an Animal Handling test you get additional questions equal to your Animal Handling Rank. While this is not actual speech, the connection with the animal is uncanny.  
**Part of the Pack**  
**Prerequisite:** One with the Beasts, Animal Handling Tag  
Your character is naturally accepted by animals. Any time you encounter a new animal, you may attempt an Animal Handling test Difficulty 1 as a Minor Action. On a success, the animal immediately considers you to be a friend. If the target is a guard animal, it does not alert its handlers to your presence.  
**Athletics**
 
**Strong Back**  
**Prerequisite:** Athletics Rank 1, Level 1  
**Maximum Rank:** 3  
Regular exercise and frequent travel with heavy equipment has resulted in a strong back. Whenever you make a Athletics test and get at least one success, add additional successes equal to your Strong Back Rank. Additionally increase your maximum carry weight by 10KG. Each time you take this perk, increase the level prerequisite by 4.
 
**Climber**  
**Prerequisite:** Strong Back  
With a strong grip and great physical form you have managed to become quite the gifted climber. You no longer suffer any penalty for not using climbing equipment and you also can reduce Difficulty of Athletic tests where you climb by 1.
 
**Iron Grip**  
**Prerequisite:** Climber, Athletics Rank 4  
On a successful Athletics test made to climb, reduce the time for free. Additionally you can never be disarmed or drop something unless you want to.
 
**One with Water**  
**Prerequisite:** Strong Back, Athletics Tag  
**Maximum Rank:** 3  
You have become a strong swimmer and can move through water with ease. When making an Athletics test for swimming you can reduce the difficulty of the test equal to your One with Water Rank. You also suffer no penalty for using Melee attacks in water.
 
**Brawler**  
**Prerequisite:** Strong Back, Athletics Tag  
With immense strength you are a dangerous presence on the battlefield. Whenever you make a Melee attack with the Strength attribute, add Knockdown Effect to the attack. If you already have the Knockdown Effect add Vicious 1.
 
**Terrifying Presence**  
**Prerequisite:** Brawler, Athletics 4  
Whenever you make a test to intimidate another creature, you can substitute Speech with Athletics if you desire.
 
**Command**  
**Leader**  
**Prerequisite:** Command Rank 2  
You speak in a clear and confident manner with little to no misinterpretation. Whenever you make a Command test you can re-roll one d20. At rank 2 you get 2 re-rolls, and at 3 you can choose which roll counts.  
**Subordinates**  
**Prerequisite:** Leader  
You are used to fighting beside creatures in your command and you end up fighting like one cohesive unit. Once per round, a creature in your command can react to you being attacked. They can either try to Defend against the attack or Attack the attacker(affected creatures must be in the same zone).  
**Inspirational Speech**  
**Prerequisite:** Command Tag, Subordinates  
**Maximum Rank:** 3  
You have become good at inspiring those around you. Whenever you are in Combat you can take new Major Action, Inspire, when you use inspire make a Command test with difficulty equal to the amount of creatures you want to inspire. On success the selected creature gains 1 CD maximum health. At higher ranks the CD increases by 1, and the level requirement increases by 2  
**Watchful Eye**  
**Prerequisite:** Leader  
You have become adept at noticing details when commanding crowds. This enables you to substitute Command when making Investigation tests when dealing with crowded areas. Also you can use a Command test to resist Thievery in crowded areas.  
**Veteran Commander**  
**Prerequisite:** Watchful Eye, Command Tag, Command Rank 4  
Every action you are a part of is simply more coordinated. Whenever you are a part of a teamwork test, even if you are not the leader, every one involved is allowed to re-roll one d20.  
**Heed My Words**  
**Prerequisite:** Leader  
As a commander you have a tendency to command even those who are not in your command. Whenever you use a Speech test to have someone do your will, substitute it with Command if you desire.  
**Commanding Presence**  
**Prerequisite:** Heed My Words  
Issuing commands has become second nature. You can use all Command tests even if the creature does not share a language with you.  
**Craft**
 
**Apprenticeship**  
**Prerequisite:** Craft Rank 1  
**Maximum Rank:** 12  
You start your crafting apprentice ship. Choose one of the workstations listed in the crafting section. Every time you use this workstation you can re-roll one d20. For each rank choose one new workstation.
 
**Crafter's Repertoire**  
**Prerequisite:** Apprenticeship  
**Maximum Rank:** 3  
As you keep working with your craft you add recipes to your repertoire. For each Craft Perk gained after this increase the rank of this perk. The rank of this perk describes what you can craft in the Crafting section. You can only get this perk once from leveling.
 
**Traveling Craftsman**  
**Prerequisite:** Crafter's Repertoire  
**Maximum Rank:** 2  
You have become good enough at your craft to work on it anywhere. The first rank of this perk allows you to craft anywhere with a kit without suffering difficulty penalties. The second rank allows you to use crafter's repertoire even when using a crafting kit.
 
**Tricks of the Trade**  
**Prerequisite:** Crafter's Repertoire, Craft Tag  
As a crafter you know a thing or two that might come to use in combat. Choose one of your workstations to learn a special ability listed under the workstation in the crafting section.
 
**Resourceful**  
**Prerequisite:** Crafter's Repertoire  
**Maximum Rank:** 2  
You have crafted for long enough to use your resources well. For each rank in this perk you can ignore one complication on your craft tests.
 
**Master Crafter**  
**Prerequisite:** Crafter's Repertoire Rank 3, Craft Tag  
**Maximum Rank:** 12  
You have mastered your craft and unlocked all recipes. You now have unlocked all recipes with the workstation you have Crafter's Repertoire Rank 3. Each Rank unlocks recipes for a new workstation.  
**Discipline**
 
**Strong Will**  
**Prerequisite:** Discipline Rank 1  
You have a strong will and better than most at staying focused. Whenever you make a Discipline test you can re-roll one d20, but must accept the new roll.
 
**Mind Fortress**  
**Prerequisite:** Strong Will, Discipline Tag  
Your mind is a difficult thing to gain access to. Whenever you succeed a struggle using the Discipline skill, each additional success also generate complications for the opponent.
 
**Debater**  
**Prerequisite:** Mind Fortress  
In a calm and collected manner you seem to be able to present facts in a persuasive way. Whenever you are trying to persuade someone to change their mind you can subsitute Discipline for Speech for that test if you desire.
 
**Lasting Focus**  
**Prerequisite:** Discipline Rank 3, Strong Will  
**Maximum Rank:** 2  
Whenever you succeed on a Focus Minor action, you can choose to keep it for rounds equal to Ranks in this perk.
 
**Great Focus**  
**Prerequisite:** Discipline Rank 4, Lasting Focus  
**Maximum Rank:** 3  
Whenever you gain at least one success on a Focus Minor Action, gain additional successes for each Rank of this perk.
 
**Sharp Mind**  
**Prerequisite:** Strong Will  
**Maximum Rank:** 2  
When you are forced to make a consentration test in order to maintain consentration, reduce the difficulty by 1 for each Rank in this perk (minimum 0)
