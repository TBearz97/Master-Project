**Counter spell**  
**Keyword:** Varied Difficulty
 
React to an enemy spell caster to cancel his magic. The difficulty of this spell is one more than the difficulty of the spell cast. The following additional effects can be applied:

- 2 IP Reflect: Reflect the spell back at another target or zone.
- 4 IP Store: Store the spell for later use. This use is a Difficulty 1 and a Major Action. Holding a spell this way requires consentration.

**Conjure Food and Drink**  
**Keyword:** Instant, Varied Difficulty
 
The caster can conjure food and drinks out of thin air by reciting selected words. The spell takes form in one or more of the following options:

- X IP Conjure Food: Conjures a selected food item where AP cost is related to rarity.
- X IP Conjure Drinks: Conjures a selected drink item where AP cost is related to rarity.
- X IP Food for the People: Conjures X extra of the selected food and/or drink item.

**Conjure Weapon**  
**Keyword:** Consentration, Varied Difficulty
 
You conjure a magical weapon out of thin air. Any attacks made with this weapon can use it's original Attribute + Skill or INT + Arcana, also damage from this weapon is considered magical. Conjure weapon can be cast in the following ways:

- 2X IP Conjure weapon: Conjuring a weapon costs X equal to it's rarity.
- Duplicate: Conjure a second weapon of same type for half the AP.
- 2X IP Deadly Weapon: Gain X additional CD for the weapon attack .
- 3X IP Potent: For each attack you roll an Effect add X Effects to that attack.

**Evoke Flame**  
**Keyword:** Consentration, Instant, Novice
 
You conjure flame in your hands to be hurled and spread in various ways. This spell deals 2 CD with the Fire Effect. Evoke flame can be cast in the following ways:

- 4 IP Flame Burst: Every creature within reach takes damage.
- 2 IP Flame Bolt: Choose one target creature to hurl the flames at.
- 6 IP Flame Ball: Choose one target zone to hurl the flames at, the ball then explode and deal damage to all in the zone.
- 6 IP Flame Wall: Choose a Line across a zone, all who start their turn within reach or enter reach take damage. Requires Consentration.
- 3 IP Flame Shield: The flames persist around you dealing damage to all who attempts to attack you. Requires Consentration.
- 2X IP Stoke the Fire: Increase the damage by X CD.

**Invoke Ice**  
**Keyword:** Consentration, Instant, Novice
 
You conjure flame in your hands to be hurled and spread in various ways. This spell deals 2CD with the Freeze Quality. Invoke Ice can be used in the following ways:

- 2 IP Ice Shards: Choose one target to hurl the ice at, this attack gains the Burst Effect.
- 2 IP Ice Spear: Choose one target to hurl the ice at, this attack gains the Piercing 1 Effect.
- 5 IP Ice Wall: Choose a Line across a zone, create a wall with 20X HP, vulnerable to Fire Damage.
- 2X IP Bitter Frost: Increase the damage by X amount.
- 3 IP Dark Ice: Gives the attack Necrotic Effect.

**Levitate**  
**Keyword:** Consentration, Varied Difficulty
 
You gain the ability to levitate. Unless otherwise stated, you merely levitate of ground and can't move unless you push yourself around. Levitate can have the following effects:

- 2 IP Levitation: You levitate above the ground, melee defense increase by 1, unless they have a weapon with reach.
- 1 IP Fly: Gain control over your movement magically.
- 5 IP Aim Higher: You levitate higher outside reach of melee attackers, weapons with reach can attack you with difficulty 1.
- 3 IP Flying with Friends: Affect X other creatures

**Teleportation**  
**Keyword:** Instant, Varied Difficulty
 
You magically teleport to a different location that you have seen. Choose one of the following options:

- 2 IP Teleport within sight.
- 4 IP Teleport within local area.
- 6 IP Teleport within region.
- 8 IP Teleport to adjacent region.
- 10 IP Teleport anywhere you've been.

In addition with your choice the spell can have the following effects:

- Travel with Company: Travel with X additional creatures. Add half location cost.
- Teleportation Circle: Teleport everyone within a zone. Add full location cost
- 1 IP Send them away: Exclude yourself from the teleportation.
- Devil's in the Detail: Teleport to a location based on description. Add half location cost.

**Necrotic Touch**  
**Keyword:** Instant, Novice
 
You channel necrotic energy into your hand giving your touch additional effects. After a successful cast you can touch a creature with the attack action dealing 3 CD with the Necrotic Effect. The spell can have the following effects:

- 2 IP Far Reach: The attack range increases by X. First spend is Reach.
- 3 IP Draining Touch: The touch also gains the Drain Effect.
- 1 IP Turn Undead: The touch also gains the Turn Undead Effect.
- 3 IP Absorb Necrosis: You can attack an ally and remove necrotic damage equal to Effects. Each point of Necrotic damage remove is an additional CD for you next attack.

**Create Illusions**  
**Keyword:** Consentration, Novice
 
Create Illusions to fool your adversaries. You can create a illusion within close range, it can only take one attack, it's difficulty to spot is 1, and it's 1 square meters unless stated otherwise. To spot the illusion a creature needs to make a INT + Investigation test. The spell can have the following effects:

- 3 IP Imagery: Create an imagery.
- 3 IP Auditory: Create a sound.
- 2X IP Grand Illusion: Increase the size by X meters.
- 1X IP Far Illusion: Increases the range by X.
- 2X IP Robust: The illusion can withstand X additional attacks.
- 1X IP Deceptive: The difficulty for spotting the illusion increases by X.
- 4 IP Mirror Illusion: Create X duplicates to increase your defense by X. If this increase causes the attack to miss that illusion is gone, thus you reduce your defense by 1.

**Temper with Time**  
**Keyword:** Consentration, Master
 
You temper with time to cause various effects. The spell can have the following effects:

- 8 IP Haste: You increase a creatures speed. For the next 3 round they gain an additional turn with initiative equal to their Agility. But the next turn after they gain only a minor action. (Can still buy 1 additional of each).
- 8 IP Slow: You slow down a creatures speed. For the next 3 rounds they gain only a minor action. (Can still buy 1 additional of each). But the next turn after they go an additional time at initiative equal to their Agility.
- 2X IP Efficiency: Reduce time to complete 1 task for X creatures.
- 3X IP Full Stop: Freeze a creature in time for X rounds. While frozen the creature is immune to all damage, effects are applied when the spell ends.

**Raise Dead**  
**Keyword:** Lasting, Skilled
 
Raise and Command Undead minions to do your bidding. Any Undead raised will remain in your control for a day. After that they need to be laid to rest or they will wander on their own. The spell can have the following effects:

- 5 IP Skeletons: Raise 2 Undead of Normal type.
- 5 IP Zombie: Raise 1 Undead of Mighty type.
- 2 IP Death Watch: Maintain control of your Undead for an additional day.
- 3 IP Treacherous Bones: Gain control of already raised Undead. If another character has control of these it becomes a struggle with you using INT + Arcana, and the character using RES + Discipline.
- 2 IP Subdue the Dead: Have Undead in your control be laid to rest.

**Enslave**  
**Keyword:** Consentration, Varied Difficulty
 
Gain control over the mind of others. This spell is always a struggle with you using INT + Arcana, and the target using RES + Discipline. On a successful struggle you can command the target to do any non-harmful and risk free act. The target makes a RES + Discipline test Difficulty 1 at the end of their turn. If successful the caster must make a consentration test, if failed all consentration spells end including this one. Additional IP can be used for the following effects:

- 2 IP Bold Words: Command the target to engage in risky activity.
- 5 IP Blood Soaked Words: Command the target to engage in a violent act.
- 10 IP True Despair: Convince the target to give up completely, having it bring it's own demise upon itself.
- 1X IP Mind Prison: Increase the difficulty of their test at the end of their turn by X.
- 4 IP Strong Will: Have the target be able to usher your commands to it's minions.
