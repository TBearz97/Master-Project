Homeland determines what kind of place shaped your character before they ended up with a life of adventure. It helps define how your character sees the world, what culture they were raised in, and what kinds of backgrounds they might have had.

**Homeland:**
Homeland is not tied to origin, but rather where you grew up. It reflects the culture that raised you, the customs you are familiar with, and the sort of life you knew before adventuring. Choosing a Homeland gives you a perk tied to that place and helps suggest suitable backgrounds.

**The Ur'Holdum Jungle**

**Perk:** Nothing Wasted. 
You know how to make use of what nature gives and what it takes.

**The Orotari Kingdom**

**Perk:** Proper Bearing
Your upbringing in a refined kingdom grants you the manners and expectations of civilized life. In formal settings, you are treated with the respect due to proper station unless you give others reason not to.

**The Frostheim Kingdom**

**Perk:** Frostborn
You can increase your Survival rating by 1.5 when it comes to exposure to cold weather.

**The Quasarian Empire**

**Perk:** Desert Adventurer
You can increase your Survival rating by 1.5 when it comes to exposure to hot weather. You can go an hour longer for every stage of Thirst and hot weather does not halve it.

**The Untamed Highlands**

**Perk:** No Safe Roads
Travel-related rules will favor your experience once those systems are in place.

**Torgana Canyons**

**Perk:** Born to Fight
Once per scene, you can completely ignore one attack that targets you.

**Calatherian Principality**

**Perk:** Looter
Whenever you successfully salvage an area, you also gain a free roll on the Oddities and Valuables table.

**Asharati Forest**

**Perk:** Animal Friend
When you encounter a creature with the Beast tag, it will never attack you on sight. Also gain 1 reroll on all Animal Handling tests.

**No Homeland**

**Perk:** Wanderer
You can become Well-Rested even when sleeping in rough conditions that would normally prevent it.

**Aratori Dominion**

**Perk:** Survivor
Necrotic damage cannot put you in a coma.

**The Obsidian Desert**

**Perk:** Scorched Resourcefulness
Foraging becomes easier anywhere.
