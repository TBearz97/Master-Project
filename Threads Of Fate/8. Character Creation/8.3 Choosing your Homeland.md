Homeland determines what roles your character might have had before they ended up with a life of adventure. Was your character a solider for the Orotari Kingdom, a trader from Quasarian Empire, an Elder from the Ur'Holdum Jungle or maybe a Dragon Knight. This section lets you get a better idea who your character is as well as determines starting equipment, language and base knowledge about the world, it also unlocks various Perks. Referencing your background in a organic matter should be rewarded with a Luck point, however this is at the GMs discretion.

**Homeland:**  
Homeland is not tied to Origin, but rather where you grew up. It reflects what culture you were brought up in and also what language you speak. Choosing a Homeland gives you a perk tied to the location, as well as a language and determines what Background you might have.

**The Ur'Holdum Jungle**
 
**Language:** Orcish, Elven
 
**Perk:** The Way of the Jungle  
You can choose one additional Tag skill among the following: Animal Handling, Survival or Medicine.
 
**Backgrounds:**  
Hunter, Acolyte, Beast Tamer, Elder or Farmer.

**The Orotari Kingdom**
 
**Language:** Elven
 
**Perk:** Elven Education  
You can choose one additional Tag skill among the following: Society, Warfare or Discipline. Also gain a language of your choice.
 
**Backgrounds:**  
Trader, Soldier, Criminal, Spy, Noble, Farmer, Sage, Acolyte, Entertainer, Artisan or Mercenary.

**The Frostheim Kingdom**
 
**Language:** Human, Elven
 
**Perk:** Frostborn  
You can increase your survival rating by 1.5 when it comes to exposure to cold weather. Also, you can reduce Freeze Effects on you by 1.
 
**Backgrounds:**  
Soldier, Farmer, Sage, Entertainer, Artisan or Pathfinder.

**The Quasarian Empire**
 
**Language:** Dwarven, Elven
 
**Perk:** Desert Adventurer  
You can increase your survival rating by 1.5 when it comes to exposure to hot weather. You can go an hour longer for every stage of Thirst and hot weather does not halve it.
 
**Backgrounds:**  
Trader, Soldier, Criminal, Spy, Noble, Farmer, Sage, Acolyte, Entertainer, Artisan, Mercenary, Trader or Pirate.

**The Untamed Highlands**
 
**Language:** Folkesome, Elven
 
**Perk:** Adaptable  
Due to the many different cities in the Untamed Highlands, you've learned to adapt to different societies. During carousing you can re-roll 1d20 on you Society test.
 
**Backgrounds:**  
Trader, Criminal, Noble, Farmer, Sage, Acolyte, Entertainer, Artisan, Mercenary or Hunter.

**Torgana Canyons**
 
**Language:** Goliath, Elven
 
**Perk:** Born to Fight  
Once per scene, you can completely ignore one attack that targets you.
 
**Backgrounds:**  
Hunter, Acolyte, Beast Tamer, Elder, Farmer, Mercenary or Gladiator.

**Calatherian Principality**
 
**Language:** Pirate, Elven
 
**Perk:** Looter  
Whenever you successfully salvage an area, you also gain a free roll on the Oddities and Valuables table.
 
**Backgrounds:**  
Trader, Pirate, Farmer, Acolyte, Entertainer, Mercenary or Artisan.

**Asharati Forest**
 
**Language:** Woodlen, Elven
 
**Perk:** Animal Friend  
When you encounter a creature with the Beast Keyword, it will never attack you on sight. Also gain 1 re-roll on all Animal Handling tests.
 
**Backgrounds:**  
Hunter, Acolyte, Beast Tamer, Elder or Farmer

**No Homeland**
 
**Language:** Elven
 
**Perk:** No Perk  
When you choose to not have a homeland, you choose to belong to a different entity. Thus you will instead gain a Perk from Background.
 
**Backgrounds:**  
Dragon Knight or Hermit.

**Aratori Dominion**
 
**Language:** Husking, Elven
 
**Perk:** Survivor  
Due to your upbringing in a hazardous land, you have learned to survive toxic and dangerous areas. When taking Hazard damage, you only take half.
 
**Backgrounds:**  
Trader, Soldier, Criminal, Spy, Noble, Farmer, Sage, Acolyte, Entertainer, Artisan or Mercenary.

**The Obsidian Desert**
 
**Language:** Ashen, Elven
 
**Perk:** Heat Resistant  
You can survive in hot weather without any penalty, and extreme hot weather is treated as hot weather.
 
**Backgrounds:**  
Dragon Knight or Hermit.
