The world of Al'otoro is home to many humanoid peoples. Collectively these civilized peoples are referred to as Kith. The nations of Al'otoro may be culturally distinct, but it is still common for different Kith to live among one another.

Each race grants one racial perk.

| **Race** | **Perk** |
| --- | --- |
| **Human** | **Good Natured**<br><br>Your kind-hearted and altruistic nature inspires and empowers your companions. When you assist another character, add your full relevant skill instead of half. |
| **Goliath** | **Unyielding**<br><br>Your people are shaped by hardship and do not break easily. When you suffer Trauma, you do not suffer the extra Concentration loss from it. |
| **High Elf** | **Social Beings**<br><br>High Elves naturally carry status and legitimacy in the eyes of many cultures. You gain +2 Renown, representing your higher standing within societies. Individual people may still feel however they wish about you. |
| **Dwarf** | **Runic Destiny**<br><br>From the moment of birth, every Dwarf bears a rune etched into their body, signifying clan, heritage, and fate. Choose one of the following runes:<br><br>**Explorer's Rune** - Whenever you roll on a loot table, roll twice and choose the outcome.<br><br>**Magister's Rune** - You know one spell for free.<br><br>**Artisan's Rune** - You gain +2 on tests when using something you made yourself. |
| **Halfling** | **Nimble Escape**<br><br>Your small frame and quick instincts make it difficult to pin you down. When moving out of melee with only one opponent threatening you, the extra AP cost does not apply. |
| **Ashborne** | **From Fire and Flame**<br><br>Forged in the heart of a volcano, your body grows magical obsidian patches. Roll a random location at character creation. That location is immune to damage while the obsidian patch remains. If that location suffers 2 Trauma, the patch is destroyed. It regrows after a week, when your Vitality is reset. |
| **Wood Elf** | **Forest Child**<br><br>Living in harmony with nature, your diet is strictly herbivorous. You cannot safely eat meat; if you do, your Max Health is reduced by 25% until you have had a full night's rest. You are immune to disease, and all other food gives you double benefit. |
| **Orc** | **We Endure**<br><br>Life is harsh, and your people are shaped by enduring it. During each full rest, you may either heal one additional Injury or reduce the duration of one disease by an additional step. |
| **Husk-Folk** | **Soulless**<br><br>Though still fully living people, Husk-Folk have been touched by necrosis so deeply that it no longer harms them as it does others. You are immune to necrotic damage. Instead, you heal the full amount of necrotic damage that would have been inflicted upon you. |
