The world of Al'otoro is one with multiple humanoid races/species. Collectively these civilized people are referred to as Kith. The Nations of Al'otoro is fairly separated with an Elven Kingdom in the center, an Dwarven Empire to the East and a Human Kingdom to the North, however it is still quite usual for all Kith to live among each other.
