As your character survives danger, overcomes enemies, and completes major goals, they grow in power and experience. This growth is represented by gaining levels.

Your character starts at level 1 unless your group agrees to begin at a higher level.

###### **When You Gain a Level**

Each time your character gains a level, apply the following changes:

- Increase your level by 1.
- Gain 6 skill points to distribute among your skills.
- Gain 1 perk.

###### **Experience Points**

Experience points (XP) are awarded by the GM for overcoming enemies, completing quests, reaching important milestones, or accomplishing meaningful goals in the story. Defeating a threat is only one way to earn XP. Escaping danger, surviving a difficult journey, solving a major problem, or bringing a conflict to a close may all justify an XP reward.

###### **Milestone Progression**

Instead of tracking XP, the GM may use milestone progression. In this method, the GM decides when the party gains a level based on story progress, major accomplishments, or important turning points in the campaign.

Milestone progression works best when the group wants advancement to follow the pace of the story rather than the number of enemies defeated or challenges overcome.
