Perks are lasting edges that shape how your character fights, thinks, survives, or uses magic. Some perks are granted by your Race, Homeland, or Background during character creation. Others are chosen later as you gain levels.

Unless a perk says otherwise, you can only take it once.

If a perk says to choose a weapon type, choose one specific weapon from the weapon lists, such as `Short Sword`, `Spear`, `Great Hammer`, `Long Bow`, or `Pistol`.

If your table uses weapon loadouts, a loadout is a prepared set of equipped weapons you can swap between. If your table does not yet use a dedicated loadout rule, treat a loadout as one readied weapon set approved by the GM.

You cannot take more than one magical identity perk unless the GM makes a specific exception.

###### **Weapon Perks**

**Weapon Proficiency**
**Requirement:** Level 2
Choose one weapon type. You gain access to that weapon's Proficiency attacks.

**Weapon Expertise**
**Requirement:** Weapon Proficiency with the chosen weapon, Level 10
Choose one weapon type in which you already have Weapon Proficiency. You gain access to that weapon's Expertise attacks.

**Weapon Mastery**
**Requirement:** Weapon Expertise with the chosen weapon, Level 15
Choose one weapon type in which you already have Weapon Expertise. You gain access to that weapon's Mastery attacks.

**Quick Swap**
**Requirement:** Weapon Proficiency, Dexterity 6
Once per round, you may swap loadouts or ready a different weapon as a free action.

**Master at Arms**
**Requirement:** Quick Swap, Level 4
You gain one additional weapon loadout.

**Dual Wielder**
**Requirement:** Dexterity 6
When fighting with a one-handed weapon in each hand, the first off-hand attack you make each round costs 1 less AP.

**Duelist**
**Requirement:** Dexterity 7, Weapon Proficiency with a one-handed melee weapon
While wielding a single one-handed melee weapon and no shield, gain +1 Deflection.

**Riposte**
**Requirement:** Duelist, Level 5
The first time each round a creature misses you with a melee attack, your next melee attack against that creature gains +1 Accuracy and deals 10% more damage.

**Accurate**
**Requirement:** Perception 6
Once each round, you may re-roll 1d20 on an attack made with a weapon you have proficiency in.

**Center of Mass**
**Requirement:** Accurate, Level 4
When you make a ranged attack against a creature within the weapon's normal range, gain +1 Accuracy.

**Bull's Eye**
**Requirement:** Center of Mass, Level 7
The first ranged attack you make each round gains +1 Crit Die size.

**Giant's Grip**
**Requirement:** Might 7
You can wield a two-handed melee weapon in one hand for the purpose of carrying or readying it, but you still need two hands to use its attacks unless another rule says otherwise.

**Hold the Line**
**Requirement:** Might 6, Constitution 6
Creatures provoke 1 additional AP to move out of your Reach.

**Heavy Armor Master**
**Requirement:** Might 7, Constitution 6, Level 5
While in Heavy Load, gain +1 Physical DT.

**Mage Slayer**
**Requirement:** Resolve 6, Level 5
When you damage a creature that currently has Concentration, reduce its Concentration by 1. This can only happen once per turn.

**Fast Hands**
**Requirement:** Dexterity 6
The first Interact action you take each round costs 1 less AP, to a minimum of 1.

###### **Magic Perks**

**Magical Student**
**Requirement:** Intelligence 6, Lore 2
Learn Theoretical spells through study and observation, gaining permanent access to all spells recorded in your spellbook without needing preparation.

**Patronage**
**Requirement:** Resolve 6
Gain spells from any school through deals, sacrifices, and narrative events, with your power shaped and potentially altered by your patron.

**Conviction**
**Requirement:** Resolve 6, Speech 2 or Lore 2
Gain a fixed set of Zealous spells based on your chosen belief or drive, changing only if your conviction fundamentally shifts.

**Ritualist**
**Requirement:** Resolve 6, Survival 2 or Lore 2
Select a limited number of Primal spells after performing a ritual, allowing you to change your available spells during a full rest.

**Innate Power**
**Requirement:** Resolve 6
Choose a magical keyword, such as Fire, Illusion, or Necrotic. You learn matching spells from any school, with a limited number of permanently known spells that grows as you level.

**Font of Mana**
**Requirement:** Resolve 7
Increase your maximum Mana by 2.

**Quiet Casting**
**Requirement:** Any magical identity perk, Level 3
Tests made to notice your spellcasting become harder unless the spell is especially loud, bright, or destructive.

**Mind Fortress**
**Requirement:** Resolve 7, Level 4
Gain +1 Concentration.

**Spellsword**
**Requirement:** Any magical identity perk, Weapon Proficiency, Level 4
Choose one weapon type you have Weapon Proficiency with. That weapon counts as a casting focus for you, and the first time each round you deal damage with it you gain 1 Mana.

**Pyromaniac**
**Requirement:** Ability to deal Fire damage
You deal 10% more damage against targets that are on fire. If a single attack or spell affects more than one target that is on fire, increase the damage by an additional 10% for each extra burning target, up to 30% total.

**Fire Starter**
**Requirement:** Ability to deal Fire damage
The first time each round you deal Fire damage, you may re-roll 1d20 on one Fire-related damage or effect roll tied to that action. If the table does not use such a roll, that attack or spell instead deals 10% more damage.

**Cryo-Expert**
**Requirement:** Ability to deal Freeze damage or use Ice magic
Creatures affected by your Ice or Freeze effects are easier to keep controlled. The first time each round you inflict Freeze, the target also loses 1 AP.

**Necrosis**
**Requirement:** Ability to deal Necrotic damage
When you deal Necrotic damage, ignore 1 Necrotic DT.

**Deliberate Strikes**
**Requirement:** Perception 6
Whenever one of your attacks scores a critical hit, gain 1 Mana.

**Crits on Crits**
**Requirement:** Deliberate Strikes, Perception 8, Level 8
When one of your Crit Dice rolls its highest value, roll one additional Crit Die of the same size and add it. Dice generated by this perk cannot generate further Crit Dice.

**Bloodlust**
**Requirement:** Might 6 or Resolve 6
When you deal 5 or more HP damage with a single attack or spell, gain 1 Mana. If you deal 10 or more HP damage, gain 2 Mana instead. You cannot gain more than 2 Mana per turn from this perk.

**Hemomancy**
**Requirement:** Resolve 6, Level 4
When you suffer 5 or more HP damage from a single source, gain 1 Mana. If you suffer 10 or more HP damage, gain 2 Mana instead. You cannot gain more than 2 Mana per turn from this perk.

###### **Leadership and Social Perks**

**War Cry**
**Requirement:** Command 2
Once per scene, when combat begins, one ally who can hear you gains +1 AP on their first turn.

**Born Leader**
**Requirement:** Command 3, Resolve 6
When you Assist an ally, they may use your full Command rank instead of half your skill bonus.

**Commanding Presence**
**Requirement:** Born Leader, Command 5, Level 6
The first time each scene you give an ally a direct order in a tense situation, their next relevant test becomes easier.

**Terrifying Presence**
**Requirement:** Might 6 or Command 2
When you try to intimidate a creature, you may use Might instead of Speech if your threat is based on force or physical menace.

**Read the Room**
**Requirement:** Insight 2
When you enter a social scene, you may ask the GM for the dominant mood, pressure, or tension in the room.

**Body Language**
**Requirement:** Insight 3
Tests to notice lies, fear, hidden hostility, or hesitation become easier for you.

**Voice of Reason**
**Requirement:** Speech 2
Once per scene, when you try to calm panic, defuse conflict, or stop a rash decision, re-roll 1d20 on the test.

**Barter**
**Requirement:** Speech 2, Society 2
When you negotiate prices, gain a 10% discount on ordinary legal goods unless a stronger rule already applies.

**Diplomat**
**Requirement:** Speech 3, Society 3
In formal negotiations, the first failed Speech or Society test you make each scene may be re-rolled.

**Inner Circle**
**Requirement:** Society 2
In courts, guilds, temples, or other structured groups, you are better at identifying who really holds influence.

**Leveraging Status**
**Requirement:** Society 3
If you have positive Renown in the current culture or faction, gain +2 on your first related social test each scene.

**Black Market**
**Requirement:** Society 2 or Stealth 2
Tests made to find illegal, hidden, or restricted goods become easier.

**I Belong Here**
**Requirement:** Society 2 or Stealth 2
When trying to pass as part of a crowd, workplace, or social function, the first test to challenge your presence becomes harder.

###### **Knowledge and Craft Perks**

**Tricks of the Trade**
**Requirement:** Craft 2
Choose one field of craft such as alchemy, smithing, bowyering, tailoring, tinkering, or writing. Tests in that field become easier.

**Traveling Craftsman**
**Requirement:** Craft 3
The penalty for crafting without a workstation is reduced for you.

**Resourceful**
**Requirement:** Craft 2 or Survival 2
Whenever the GM would rule that you can salvage useful materials, you recover a little more than usual.

**Master Crafter**
**Requirement:** Craft 4, Tricks of the Trade, Level 6
Choose one craft field you selected for Tricks of the Trade. Re-roll 1d20 on the first related Craft test you make each scene.

**Eye for Detail**
**Requirement:** Investigation 2
Tests to notice hidden compartments, tampering, weak points, or concealed details become easier.

**Cryptographer**
**Requirement:** Investigation 2, Lore 2
Tests to decode ciphers, break simple codes, and understand encoded writing become easier.

**Polyglot**
**Requirement:** Lore 2
Choose one culture, region, or people. Tests to understand their customs, idioms, coded references, and written materials become easier. You may take this perk multiple times.

**Bestiary**
**Requirement:** Lore 2
The first time you encounter a creature type, you may ask the GM for one useful fact about its habits, weaknesses, or dangers.

**Historian**
**Requirement:** Lore 2
Tests involving major historical events, dynasties, ruins, and old conflicts become easier.

**Theologian**
**Requirement:** Lore 2
Tests involving gods, cults, doctrine, sacred practices, and religious history become easier.

**Anatomist**
**Requirement:** Medicine 2 or Lore 2
When you attack a living creature, the first hit you score each round deals 10% more damage.

**Cartographer**
**Requirement:** Navigation 2
When mapping a route, charting an area, or returning by a known path, re-roll 1d20 on the test.

**Seafarer**
**Requirement:** Navigation 2
Navigation tests at sea become easier.

###### **Survival and Field Perks**

**Strong Back**
**Requirement:** Athletics 1
Increase your maximum Carry Weight by 10 kg.

**One with Water**
**Requirement:** Athletics 2
Tests for swimming become easier, and fighting in water hinders you less than normal.

**Charge**
**Requirement:** Athletics 3
If you move at least 3 hexes in a straight line before making a melee attack, that attack deals 10% more damage.

**Iron Grip**
**Requirement:** Athletics 4
Tests for climbing become easier, and you may re-roll 1d20 when resisting being disarmed.

**Evasive**
**Requirement:** Dexterity 6
When you take Guard, gain +1 additional Deflection against the first attack that targets you before your next turn.

**Feline Grace**
**Requirement:** Dexterity 6, Athletics 2
Jumping, leaping, and landing safely become easier for you. Whenever you take falling damage, reduce the final damage by 10%.

**Mobile**
**Requirement:** Feline Grace, Level 4
Moving through difficult terrain costs 1 less AP for you, to a minimum of 1.

**Faithful Companions**
**Requirement:** Animal Handling 2
You gain one animal companion approved by the GM. It is a loyal Beast that is weaker than a player character but useful in travel, scouting, or support.

**Strong Companions**
**Requirement:** Faithful Companions, Animal Handling 4, Level 5
Choose one of your animal companions. It becomes stronger, tougher, or more capable in a way agreed with the GM.

**Part of the Pack**
**Requirement:** Animal Handling 2
When you first encounter a non-hostile or uncertain Beast, you may attempt to calm it as a free social gesture before violence begins.

**Danger Detector**
**Requirement:** Survival 2 or Insight 2
The first test you make each scene to detect an ambush, trap, or obvious environmental hazard becomes easier.

**Hunter**
**Requirement:** Survival 2
Tests to track beasts, follow signs, and pursue prey become easier.

**Forager**
**Requirement:** Survival 2
Foraging takes less time for you, and poor conditions hinder you less than normal.

**Survivalist**
**Requirement:** Survival 4
You can become Well-Rested in harsher conditions than most characters, so long as the location is not actively dangerous.

**Heavy Sleeper**
**Requirement:** Constitution 5
Once per full rest, you may ignore one source of interrupted sleep unless it is directly harmful.

**Know the Way**
**Requirement:** Navigation 3, Survival 2
When leading a journey through land you have already crossed, the first Navigation or Survival test related to routefinding each day becomes easier.

###### **Medicine and Stealth Perks**

**Field Medic**
**Requirement:** Medicine 2
Tests to stabilize, bandage, or treat injuries in the field become easier.

**Healer**
**Requirement:** Medicine 3 or any magical identity perk
Whenever you restore HP to another creature, add +1 healing.

**Defiance**
**Requirement:** Resolve 2
Once per scene, re-roll 1d20 on a test to resist fear, despair, coercion, or magical influence.

**Lasting Focus**
**Requirement:** Resolve 4
The first time each scene you would lose Concentration, reduce that loss by 1.

**Clarity**
**Requirement:** Intelligence 6 or Resolve 6
The first Lore, Investigation, or Insight test you make each scene may be re-rolled.

**From the Shadows**
**Requirement:** Stealth 2
The first attack you make against a creature that has not noticed you gains +1 Accuracy.

**Ghost**
**Requirement:** From the Shadows, Stealth 4
Tests made to notice you become harder while you are hidden and stationary.

**Pickpocket**
**Requirement:** Sleight of Hand 2
Tests to lift, plant, or palm a small object become easier.

**Deadly Ambush**
**Requirement:** From the Shadows, Level 4
The first damaging hit you make against a creature that has not noticed you deals 20% more damage.

**Vanish**
**Requirement:** Ghost, Level 7
Once per scene after you break line of sight, you may immediately attempt to hide without spending additional AP.

**Infiltrator**
**Requirement:** Stealth 2, Sleight of Hand 2
Tests to bypass locks, simple security, or unnoticed entry become easier.
