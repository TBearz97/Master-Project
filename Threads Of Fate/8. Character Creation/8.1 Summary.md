When making a character you will go through a series of steps that determine who they are, what they can do, and how they begin play. Every character starts at level 1 unless your GM says otherwise.

The steps to creating a character are as follows:

- Choose your Race.
- Choose your Homeland.
- Choose your Background.
- Distribute your Attributes.
- Distribute your Skills.
- Choose your Starting Equipment.
- Write your Backstory.

After character creation, your character grows through the advancement rules in `8.9 Character Advancement` and the perk rules in `8.12 Perks`.
