When making a character you will be going through a few steps in order to determine their attributes, skills, backstory, perks and equipment. Every character starts at level 1, unless your GM says otherwise.  
The steps to creating a character are as follows:

- Choosing your Race
- Choosing your Homeland and Background
- Distributing Attributes
- Distributing Skills
- Calculate your Derived Statistics
- Starting Equipment
- Writing a backstory
