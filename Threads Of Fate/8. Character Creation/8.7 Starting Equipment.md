Starting equipment is chosen with Gold. At character creation, each player begins with 600 Gold to spend on weapons, armor, clothing, tools, consumables, and other gear. This Gold represents the equipment, savings, gifts, and practical supplies your character has managed to gather before the campaign begins.

If a player does not want to shop item by item, they may instead choose one of the starter packs below. Starter packs are fixed, ready-made loadouts that also include whatever Gold remains from the standard 600 Gold starting amount.

###### **Standard Starting Gold**

- 600 Gold
