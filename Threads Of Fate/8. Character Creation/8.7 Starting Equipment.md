As our character sets out to explore the vast and wondrous lands of Al'otoro, they do not underestimate the importance of being prepared. With a keen eye for adventure and a heart full of courage, they gather the most essential and basic equipment needed to survive in this foreign terrain. A sturdy backpack is slung over their shoulder, containing a waterskin, a bedroll, and enough rations to last several days. Their trusty walking staff is in hand, a tool for traversing rugged landscapes and warding off any unwanted creatures that may cross their path. With their leather boots firmly laced and their simple clothing suited for the weather, our character is ready to embark on a journey full of wonder, danger, and excitement.
 
**Base Equipment:**

- Backpack, Small
- Bedroll
- Waterskin, Filled with Clean Water
- Bread x 5
- Commoner's Clothing
- 20 Gold
 
**You can also choose two of the following:**

- Choose one Perk.
- Choose one Weapon of Rarity 2 or lower.
- Choose one Tag Skill.
- A Medicine Bag.
- Three Pieces of Armor of Rarity 2 or lower.
- A Crafting Kit.
- A Lockpick Set.
 
After all equipment has been noted, also gain proficiency in any weapon gained during character creation.
