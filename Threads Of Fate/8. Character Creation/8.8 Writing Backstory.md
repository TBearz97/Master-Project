Now that all the stats, and meta parts of the character is done, it's time to delve a little further into the role playing of it all. Using the choices you have made for your character write a short little story about your character. This should be anything from a short sweet summary to a full page of information. Writing a backstory is important for a roleplaying focused table, as it helps you, the GM and your fellow players get a better understanding of your character and it makes it easier for you to think of choices your character would make.
