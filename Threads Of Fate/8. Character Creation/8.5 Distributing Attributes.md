Every creature's capabilities are defined by six attributes. They describe a character's physical and mental strengths and shape how they solve problems. The six attributes are Might, Dexterity, Constitution, Perception, Intelligence, and Resolve.

Each attribute ranges from 1 to 10, where 1 is very poor and 10 is exceptional. When creating a character, every attribute starts at 5. You have 5 points to spend to increase your attributes, and each point spent increases a single attribute by 1. You may also reduce an attribute down to 1 in order to gain more points to spend elsewhere.

## **Might (MGT)**
Might reflects raw force. It is the strength behind a crushing blow, the power needed to haul equipment across a battlefield, and the bodily force that lets a character impose themselves on the world.

Characters with high Might tend to fight directly, carry more into danger, and endure heavier burdens without losing effectiveness.

## **Dexterity (DEX)**
Dexterity measures speed, balance, flexibility, and bodily control. It governs how quickly you can act, how easily you avoid incoming attacks, and how sharply your body reacts to sudden danger.

Characters with high Dexterity take more actions in combat, are harder to hit, and respond more effectively to hazards and area effects.

Reflex is derived from both Dexterity and Perception.

## **Constitution (CON)**
Constitution measures bodily resilience. It is the deep reserve that lets a character remain standing, survive blood loss, and hold together when the body is pushed beyond what should be survivable.

Characters with high Constitution are harder to put down and can survive far more punishment before death takes them.

## **Perception (PER)**
Perception measures awareness and precision. It is the ability to notice a weakness in an enemy's stance, recognize danger before it strikes, and place attacks exactly where they matter most.

Characters with high Perception see what others miss, turn that awareness into accuracy, and land deadlier critical hits.

Reflex is derived from both Dexterity and Perception.

## **Intelligence (INT)**
Intelligence reflects memory, reason, and applied thought. It is the attribute behind clever planning, understanding systems, and shaping knowledge into something useful.

Characters with high Intelligence solve problems by understanding them. They are also better at applying secondary effects and learning from what they encounter.

Mind is derived from both Intelligence and Resolve.

## **Resolve (RES)**
Resolve reflects inner drive, self-control, and force of spirit. It is what lets a person stay focused under pressure, endure fear, and continue acting when lesser people would falter.

Characters with high Resolve generate more Mana, hold Concentration more reliably, and resist attacks against the mind.

Mind is derived from both Intelligence and Resolve.
