Backgrounds describe the life your character lived before adventuring. Each background grants two fixed skills, rerolls with those skills in fitting situations, and one small perk. Backgrounds should help explain how your character solves problems and what sort of life shaped them.

The perk from a background should usually reflect habit, training, or social position rather than dramatic supernatural power. Backgrounds are meant to give identity and practical edges, not define your whole build.
