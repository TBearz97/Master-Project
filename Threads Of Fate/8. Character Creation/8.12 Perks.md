Perks are special bonuses that you can obtain to boost your character’s attributes, skills, give them a unique edge, or even a brand new ability. Perks are often tied to attributes or skills, enhancing them, or providing you with a totally new ability in a specific circumstance. Some perks can be chosen more than once—the number of ranks each perk has determines how many times you can take it. Each rank increases the effect of the perk, whether that’s providing the same bonus again, or increasing the effect of the ability the perk gives you. Perks can be mixed, combined and applied in which ever way you want to maximize benefit unless otherwise specifically stated.
