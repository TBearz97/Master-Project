**Counter spell**  
**Keyword:** Instant  
**Difficulty:** Enemy Cast Difficulty + 1  
**Base Cast:**  
React to an enemy spell caster to cancel his magic. The difficulty of this spell is one more than the difficulty of the spell cast.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (Difficulty 2) Reflect: Reflect the spell back at another target or zone.
- (Difficulty 3) Store: Store the spell for later use. This use is a Difficulty 1 and a Major Action. Holding a spell this way requires consentration.

**Conjure Food and Drink**  
**Keyword:** Instant  
**Base Cast:**  
The caster can conjure food and drinks out of thin air by reciting selected words.

- Conjure Food: Conjures a selected food item where Difficulty is related to rarity.
- Conjure Drinks: Conjures a selected drink item where Difficulty is related to rarity.

**Variety Cast:**

- (Difficulty 1) Food for the People: Conjures X extra of the selected food and/or drink item.

**Conjure Weapon**  
**Keyword:** Concentration  
**Base Cast:**  
You conjure a magical weapon out of thin air. Any attacks made with this weapon can use it's original Attribute + Skill or INT + Arcana, also damage from this weapon is considered magical. Conjuring a weapon has a difficulty equal to it's rarity.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (+ Difficulty Base Cast/2) Duplicate: Conjure a second weapon of same type.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Deadly Weapon: Gain 1 additional CD for the weapon attack .
- (+ Difficulty 2) Potent: Whenever you attack with the conjured weapon, each Effect now counts as 1 damage + 2 Effects.

**Evoke Flame**  
**Keyword:** Instant  
**Difficulty:** Target Defense  
**Optimal Range:** M  
**Base Cast:**  
You conjure flame in your hands to be hurled at a target. This spell deals 5 CD with the Persistent(Fire) Effect.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (+ Difficulty 1) Flame Burst: The attack range is reduced to Reach, but also gains 1 CD and Blast Quality.
- (+ Difficulty 2) Flame Ball: The attack gains 3 CD and Blast Quality.
- (+ Difficulty 2 ~~Target Defense~~) Flame Wall: Instead of a target, choose a Line across a zone. All who start their turn within reach or enter reach take damage. Requires Concentration.
- (+ Difficulty 1 ~~Target Defense~~) Flame Shield: Instead of a target, the flames persist around you dealing damage to all who attempts to attack you. Requires Concentration.

**Invoke Ice**  
**Keyword:** Instant  
**Difficulty:** Target Defense  
**Optimal Range:** L  
**Base Cast:**  
You invoke ice in your hands to be hurled at a target. This spell deals 6 CD with the Freeze Effect.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (+ Difficulty 1) Ice Shards: Choose one target to hurl the ice at, this attack gains the Burst Effect.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Ice Spear: Choose one target to hurl the ice at, this attack gains the Piercing 1 Effect.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Dark Ice: Gives the attack Necrotic Effect.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Frost Nova: The attack range is reduced to Reach, but also Grappling Effect and Blast Quality.
- (+ Difficulty 2 ~~Target Defense~~) Ice Wall: Choose a Line across a zone, create a wall with 20HP, vulnerable to Fire Damage. Requires Concentration.

**Levitate**  
**Keyword:** Concentration  
**Difficulty:** 1  
**Base Cast:**  
You gain the ability to levitate of ground and can't move unless you push yourself around. While levitating melee defense increase by 1, unless attacked by a weapon with reach.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (+ Difficulty 1) Fly: Gain control over your movement magically.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Aim Higher: You levitate higher outside reach of melee attackers, weapons with reach can attack you with difficulty increase +1.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Flying with Friends: Cast on another creature within close range instead.

**Teleportation**  
**Keyword:** Instant  
**Difficulty:** 1  
**Optimal Range:** M  
**Base Cast:**  
You magically teleport to a zone within range you can see.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (+ Difficulty 1) Blink: Teleport anywhere within sight. Maximum Long Range.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Travel with Company: Travel with one additional creature that is within Reach.
- (+ Difficulty 3) Mass Teleport: Teleport everyone within a zone.
- (+ Difficulty Target Defense) Send them away: You don't teleport yourself, but rather another creature within Reach.
- (+ Difficulty 4) Devil's in the Detail: Teleport to a location based on description.
- (+ Difficulty X) Teleportation Circle: Chose a number between 1-3 to replace X. After concentrating for 10 minutes teleport up to 8 creatures anywhere within 5*X hexes on the travel map.

**Necrotic Touch**  
**Keyword:** Instant  
**Difficulty:** Target Defense  
**Optimal Range:** Reach  
**Base Cast:**  
You channel necrotic energy into your hand. After a successful cast you can touch a creature as a attack dealing 3 CD with the Necrotic Effect.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (+ Difficulty 1) Draining Touch: The touch also gains the Drain Effect.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Turn Undead: The touch also gains the Turn Undead Effect.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Absorb Necrosis: You can target an ally and remove necrotic damage equal to Effects, this does not damage the ally. Each point of Necrotic damage remove is an additional CD for you next attack.

**Temper with Time**  
**Keyword:** Concentration  
**Difficulty:** 3  
**Optimal Range:** C  
**Base Cast:**  
You temper with time around a creature to speed them up or slow them down.

- Quickened: You increase a creatures speed. For the next 3 round they gain an additional turn with initiative equal to their Agility. But the next turn after they are slowed.
- Slow: You slow down a creatures speed. For the next 3 rounds they gain only a minor action. (Can still buy 1 additional of each). But the next turn after they are quickened.

**Variety Cast:**

- (+ Difficulty 1) Full Stop: Instead of slowing them you freeze a creature in time. While frozen the creature stacks up damage and Effects to be applied when the spell ends.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Elegant Speed: A sped up target can now take 3 Major Actions and Minor Actions if they spend the IP for it.

**IP Spend:**

- 2 IP: Increase the number of rounds by 1.

**Create Illusions**  
**Keyword:** Concentration  
**Difficulty:** 1  
**Optimal Range:** C  
**Base Cast:**  
Create Illusions to fool your adversaries. It's only visual, it has 1HP 0 Defense, with a PER + Investigation test Difficulty 1 a creature can spot it's fake and it's 1 square meter.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (+ Difficulty 1) Auditory: Create a sound.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Robust: The illusion can survive one attack without being destroyed or detected.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Grand Illusion: The illusion is 1 square meters. Repeatable.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Mirror Illusion: Create 2 duplicates to increase your defense by 2. If this increase causes the attack to miss that illusion, then it's gone, thus you reduce your defense by 1.

**IP Spend:**

- 1 IP: Increase detection Difficulty by 1.

**Raise Dead**  
**Keyword:** Lasting  
**Difficulty:** 1  
**Base Cast:**  
Raise and Command Undead minions to do your bidding. When you cast this you raise one creature with the Undead Keyword of Normal type at your level or lower. Any Undead raised will remain in your control for a day. After that they need to be laid to rest or they will wander on their own. You can also use the base cast of this spell to add another day to one of your controlled undead. You can only use the base cast of this spell to raise One Undead each scene.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (+ Difficulty 2) Competence: The summoned creature is Mighty/Notable.
- (+ Difficulty 3) Of Legends: The summoned creature is Legendary/Major.
- (+ Difficulty X) Death Watch: If you have control of more than one Undead, the test to maintain control of them increases by each additional Undead you control.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Treacherous Bones: Gain control of already raised Undead. If another character has control of these it becomes a struggle with you using INT + Arcana, and the character using RES + Discipline.

**IP Spend:**

- 2 IP: Have Undead in your control be laid to rest. This can be done at any point.
- 3 IP: Extra Normal creature.
- 5 IP: Extra Mighty/Notable creature.
- 6 IP: Extra Legendary/Major creature.

**Enslave**  
**Keyword:** Concentration  
**Difficulty:** Struggle  
**Optimal Range:** C  
**Base Cast:**  
Gain control over the mind of others. This spell is always a struggle with you using INT + Arcana, and the target using RES + Discipline. On a successful struggle you can command the target to do any non-harmful and risk free act. The target makes a RES + Discipline test Difficulty 1 at the end of their turn. If successful the caster must make a consentration test, if failed all consentration spells end including this one.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (+ Difficulty 1) Bold Words: Command the target to engage in risky activity.
- (+ Difficulty 2) Blood Soaked Words: Command the target to engage in a violent act.
- (+ Difficulty 3) True Despair: Convince the target to give up completely, having it bring it's own demise upon itself.
- (+ Difficulty 1) Strong Will: Have the target be able to usher your commands to it's minions, these commands can only be of the same risk level you control the main target.

**IP Spend:**

- 2 IP: Increase the Difficulty to break free from the spell.
