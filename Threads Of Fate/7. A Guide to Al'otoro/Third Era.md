Early in the Third Era several forms of Magic is discovered. Priests discover Religious Magic, Universities discover Theoretical Magic and Hermits, Wood Elves and Orc are rumored to wield several forms of Nature Magic.
 
Dwarves invent Firearms.
 
Mid Third Era, the High Kings starts a campaign to reclaim the land the Aratori Dominion took from then during the Second Era. The war was brutal and it's commonly believed that at the end, the High Elves we're mostly going town to town slaughtering villages.
