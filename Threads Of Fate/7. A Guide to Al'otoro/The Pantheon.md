THE LUMINESCENT
 
God of Subterranean Light and Illusions
 
This is the God of the World Below. In a dark and dangerous world, The Luminescent protects it's inhabitants by bringing light to their world. The Luminescent is also believed to be a role-model for charity, believing in helping others and the patience to endure the world below. The Gods form a court where they judge the dead to heaven or hell. The dead are judged by the 7 heavenly virtues and 7 deadly sins, as well as their devotion to a God. Devotion to a God might grant you access to an afterlife you otherwise wouldn't get.  
Which afterlife is ideal depends on the creature, a creature that values one or more of the deadly sins would thrive in Hell and a virtuous creature will find peace in heaven.
 
Example: A kith down on his luck must result in crime, greed and other sinful behavior. However the kith places more value in virtuous behavior, but circumstances makes it difficult to act in accordance with those values. This means the Gods would most likely place him in hell even though he desires Heaven. Devotion to a God might earn them a place in heaven.
 
While the Gods come to a decision the dead are placed in purgatory, a empty isolated place outside space and time. How long you spend in purgatory varies on how long the Gods spend arguing over your where to place you. A extended time in purgatory increases the time you can be resurrected however it also leaves you prone to madness. Some are said to be stuck in purgatory due to the Gods not being able to agree.

**Tale of Knowledge and Secrets**  
_There was once a young scribe, a scribe that had an unending appetite for knowledge. He came to our world to study it, everything he saw he picked apart and analyzed, wrote it down and moved on to the next. It didn't take long before he had the most impressive library in the universe. But after a while his brother came to visit him, his brother did not agree with his methods. His brother was in the opinion that no one should possess this amount of knowledge, a world where no stone is unturned is a sad and grey world. Life isn't life if you have the solution to every problem. The brother then decided to curse the scribe. A curse that he later forgot how to undo. The scribe was now able to continue researching to his hearts content, however he could no longer determine facts from falsehoods. An thus the brother began his work, to hide and misplace information, to conjure and spread miss information. An creating a world of endless mystery. This is the tale of the scribe that became the Erudite and his brother the Obscured._
 
Seven Heavenly Virtues:
 
1. Charity - The selflessness to give to others, even when it may be difficult or inconvenient.
2. Valor - The courage to face danger, even when the odds are against you.
3. Humility - The quality of being humble and modest, and the ability to acknowledge one's limitations and mistakes.
4. Contentment - The state of being satisfied with what one has, without the desire for others possesions.
5. Patience - The ability to remain calm and composed in the face of adversity or provocation.
6. Kindness - The compassion for others, and the desire to bring happiness and fulfillment to those around you.
7. Moderation - The practice of self-restraint and temperance in one's actions and desires.
 
Seven Deadly Sins:
 
1. Greed - The insatiable desire for wealth and material possessions.
2. Cowardice - The lack of courage or bravery in the face of danger.
3. Pride - The arrogance that leads to a belief in one's superiority.
4. Envy - The desire for that which belongs to others, whether it be wealth, power, or status.
5. Wrath - The anger that fuels revenge and destruction.
6. Inconsideration - A failure to take others' needs or feelings into account.
7. Hedonism - The excessive indulgence in physical pleasures such as sex, food, or drink, without regard for consequences or the well-being of oneself or others.
