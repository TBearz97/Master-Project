High King Altoro founds of the Orotari Kingdom and the Capital Otoro. He named the world Al'otoro and started tracking the years beginning at 0 the year he was born. Also declaring that when his son eventually becomes High King a new Era shall begin. He led a campaign of peace and establishing a land for each evolved race and faction. He rallied the raced behind a campaign against the dragons. During his last years he worked towards a treaty with the dragons. Legend has it when he died he became The Sentinel.
