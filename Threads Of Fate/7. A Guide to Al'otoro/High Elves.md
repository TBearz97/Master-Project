High Elven culture is driven by a cast system, where naturally they see themselves on top. Their behavior varies wildly based on the species they are interacting with. Most species are considered "good" in their eyes and they treat these races with various amounts of kindness but always more kindness than what your average person would do. While the "bad" races are treated in the other extreme, with slightly varying degrees. Most are considered livestock with no real person behind them, while the worst ones aren't even considered suited for that. However, they are not oblivious to the ways of other cultures, and the respect they show to the "good" races outweigh the distain they show towards the "bad" ones, meaning if a "bad" person is introduced as a friend of a "good" person, the elves will simply considered the "bad" one to be "good". This is because their kindness wouldn't allow them to potentially offend the "good" one, even if it means treating the "bad" one "correctly".
