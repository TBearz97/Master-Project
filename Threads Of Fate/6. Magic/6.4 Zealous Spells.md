**Aura of Conviction**
**Tag:** Concentration, Zealous
**Difficulty:** 2
**Base Cast:**
Create an aura that follows you and fills your current area. The aura's exact expression is shaped by your belief, oath, or divine allegiance.

- Aura of Mercy: Divide 4 CD of healing among other creatures in the aura.
- Aura of Judgment: Creatures within the aura that are already on the brink of death may be finished outright.
- Aura of Revelation: Creatures within the aura are revealed.
- Aura of Veils: Name an object and all instances of that object become harder to notice within the aura.
- Aura of Beasts: Creatures with the Beast tag regard you as an ally.
- Aura of Insight: Intelligence tests become easier within the aura.
- Aura of Craft: Weapons within the aura become more reliable, and Craft tests within the aura become easier.
- Aura of Wrath: Creatures damaged within the aura strike back harder on their next attack.
- Aura of the Sentinel: Creatures have a harder time moving away from the center of the aura.
- Aura of Flame: Weapons within the aura gain Fire.
- Aura of Sand: You may strengthen or calm a sandstorm while the aura lasts.

**Variety Cast:**
- (3 Mana) Center on Other: Cast the aura on a specific area or another creature within Close range.
- (3 Mana) Increased Influence: Increase the scale of the aura.

**Divine Smite**
**Tag:** Instant, Weapon
**Difficulty:** 1
**Base Cast:**
Brandish your weapon with sacred power for the next attack. The attack deals its normal damage plus 2 CD.

**Variety Cast:**
- (2 Mana) Forceful Smite: The attack pushes the target backward and punishes collisions.
- (2 Mana) Necrotic Smite: The attack also gains Necrotic.
- (2 Mana) Brandishing Smite: The attack leaves a sacred mark tied to your belief that others of that faith can sense.

**Faithful Light**
**Tag:** Instant, Harmless
**Difficulty:** 1
**Optimal Range:** C
**Base Cast:**
Project a line of sacred light toward a target. When it reaches a creature, that creature regains 2 CD Health.

**Variety Cast:**
- (3 Mana) Close Wounds: The light also removes one Injury.
- (2 Mana) Cure Corruption: The light heals Necrotic damage.
- (2 Mana) Blinding Light: The light instead deals 4 CD damage and applies Stun.

**Blessing**
**Tag:** Concentration, Harmless
**Difficulty:** 1
**Optimal Range:** Reach
**Base Cast:**
Bless another creature with divine favor. Increase one of that creature's skills by 2, up to 8.

**Variety Cast:**
- (1 Mana) Common Blessing: The target gains +1 Concentration while the spell lasts.
- (3 Mana) Divine Attribute: Choose an attribute. The target gains one reroll on tests using that attribute while the spell lasts.
- (4 Mana) Divine Inspiration: The target gains Mana or AP, as appropriate to the blessing.

**Words of Power**
**Tag:** Instant
**Difficulty:** 3
**Optimal Range:** C
**Base Cast:**

**Weapon of Faith**
**Tag:** Lasting, Zealous
**Base Cast:**
Summon your weapon of faith. What form this weapon takes is determined by your belief. Unlike Conjure Weapon, you do not freely choose the weapon at cast time. It is the sacred weapon associated with your conviction, oath, or divine allegiance.

While summoned, the weapon functions like a normal weapon from the equipment lists, but counts as magical. The exact weapon granted depends on your belief. Some beliefs grant only one sacred weapon, while others allow a small set of valid sacred weapons.

**Variety Cast:**
- (2 Mana) Sanctified Force: Increase the weapon's damage for a short time.
- (2 Mana) Guiding Hand: The weapon helps strike true against the faith's enemies.
- (4 Mana) Living Testament: The weapon expresses a stronger miracle tied to your belief.

**Watchful Presence**
**Tag:** Concentration, Harmless
**Difficulty:** 2
**Optimal Range:** C
**Base Cast:**
Place a watchful presence over a creature. The next time that creature would fall to 0 Health, it instead returns to a small amount of Health after the attack resolves.

**Variety Cast:**
- (2 Mana) Retaliation: When the target drops below half Health, it may immediately strike back.
- (3 Mana) Invigoration: When the target drops below a threshold, it heals 4 CD.
- (3 Mana) Protection: When the target drops below a threshold, it gains temporary DT.
- (2 Mana) Obscured: When the target is badly hurt, it briefly becomes hidden.
- (2 Mana) Surrounding Sands: When the target is nearly defeated, a protective wall rises around it.

**Condemn**
**Tag:** Concentration, Mind
**Difficulty:** Target Mind
**Base Cast:**

**Variety Cast:**

**Cleansing Flame**
**Tag:** Concentration, Fire
**Difficulty:** Target Defense + 1
**Optimal Range:** M
**Base Cast:**
Engulf a target in a cleansing flame that persists while you maintain Concentration. At the start of each of your turns, the target takes 4 CD damage with Burning and Intense.

**Variety Cast:**
- (4 Mana) Make Them Suffer: Increase the ongoing damage.
- (4 Mana) Purify: If the target accumulates enough Injuries while the spell lasts, it dies.
- (4 Mana) Jumping Flame: If the target dies, move the flame to another nearby target.
- (6 Mana) Burn the Soul: This damage ignores all DT.
