**Aura of the gods**  
**Keywords:** Concentration, Oathsworn  
**Difficulty:** 2  
**Base Cast:**  
Create an aura that follows you and covers your current area and creates a magical effect based on your oathsworn God. The area is a Circle with Scale 1.

- Aura of the Quasar: Roll 4 CD. Divide the result between each other creature inside your zone. If you heal less than amount of creatures choose who to heal.
- Aura of the Soulless: Any creatures within the aura that has three or more injuries instantly dies.
- Aura of the Luminescent: Any creatures at any time within this aura is revealed.
- Aura of the Obscured: Name an object and every instance of said object is hidden within the aura.
- Aura of the Ferocious: Any creature with the Beast Keyword will see you as an ally.
- Aura of the Erudite: Reduce the difficulty of Intelligence tests by 1.
- Aura of the Artisan: All weapons within the aura gains the reliable Quality. Creatures within the aura can also ignore one complication on Craft tests.
- Aura of the Vengeful: If an creature within the aura was damaged during this turn, their next attack gains the Vicious Effect.
- Aura of the Sentinel: All creatures within this aura has reduced move speed away from the center of the aura(if the aura is centered on you or someone else, that person is not affected). Any creature that takes the Move minor action gets pulled into reach of the center of the aura. Any creature that takes the Sprint Action can only reach the adjacent zone at the edge.
- Aura of the Fiery: All weapons within the aura gains the Fire Effect.
- Aura of the Hearth of the Sand: Cause or dampen a sand storm as a Free Action once on subsequent turns.

**Variety Cast:**

- (3 Mana) Center on Other: Cast an Aura that covers an specific area or centers on another creature within Close range.
- (3 Mana) Increased Influence: Increases the Scale by 1.

**Divine Smite**  
**Keyword:** Instant  
**Difficulty:** 1  
**Base Cast:**  
As a Minor Action you brandish your weapon with divine power for your next attack. The attack deals the weapons normal damage and effects plus an extra 2 CD and gets the Vicious 1 effect.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (2 Mana) Forceful Smite: The attack pushes the target back 2 hexes. If target hits a obstacle, the target and whatever is blocking it takes 2 CD damage per hex not moved.
- (2 Mana) Necrotic Smite: The attack gains the necrotic effect.
- (2 Mana) Brandishing Smite: The attack leaves an imprint on the target limb that cannot be removed. The imprint takes form of the symbol of your god, any followers of your god can sense when an imprint is within the same scene. The only thing that can remove this imprint is if the target is forgiven by the chosen god.

**Faithful Light**  
**Keyword:** Instant  
**Difficulty:** 1  
**Optimal Range:** C  
**Base Cast:**  
Through prayer you shine a light that emits from you in a straight line towards the target. When the light reaches another creature it regains 2 CD HP. This cannot go through a different target.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (3 Mana) Close wounds: When the light reaches another creature it can remove one injury.
- (2 Mana) Cure corruption: When the light reaches another creature you can heal necrotic damage. This only removes the necrotic damage and does not increase max HP or current HP.
- (2 Mana) Blinding Light: The light instead deals 4 CD damage with Stun Effect and Ray 2 Quality.

**Blessing**  
**Keyword:** Concentration, Harmless  
**Difficulty:** 1  
**Optimal Range:** Reach  
**Base Cast:**  
Your pray for another creature giving it benefits and increased chance of success. You increase one skill of another creature by 2. This can exceed the normal limit of 6, but no higher that 8.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (1 Mana) Common Blessing: You pray to a god and asks them to watch over a person, homestead or something else. While Concentrating the target has 1 extra Luck Point.
- (3 Mana) Divine Attribute: You chose a Attribute, the target then has 1 reroll on all tests with chosen attribute.
- (4 Mana) Divine Inspiration: Increase the max of Party's IP pool by 2.

**Words of Power**  
**Keyword:** Instant  
**Difficulty:** 3  
**Optimal Range:** C  
**Base Cast:**  
Speak a Word of Power that legends say descended from Gods. Your defense increases by 2 until the end of your next turn.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (8 Mana) Kill: You focus on another creature as you speak this word. If the target creature has two or more injuries it dies.
- (6 Mana) Heal: You focus on another creature as you speak this word, the target creature restores all injuries suffered.
- (6 Mana) Pain: You focus on another creature as you speak this word, you deal 10 CD damage with the Intense Quality.
- (8 Mana) Hope: You utter the word quietly for yourself. You increase the maximum of Party's IP pool by 2 and fills it at the start of each of your turns. Requires Concentration.

**Watchful Presence**  
**Keyword:** Concentration, Harmless  
**Difficulty:** 2  
**Optimal Range:** C  
**Base Cast:**  
Cast on a creature within range. While being watched, when you reach 0 HP you go back to 10% HP. This happens after an attack has completed and can't be cast on unconscious or dead creatures.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (2 Mana) Retaliation: When you fall below 50% HP, make an attack targeting the most recent attacker within reach.
- (3 Mana) Invigoration: When you fall below 25% HP, heal 4 CD.
- (3 Mana) Protection: When you fall below 75% HP, gain 2 DT to all damage types for the next 3 rounds.
- (2 Mana) Obscured: The next time you take damage become invisible for 3 rounds or until you interact with another creature.
- (2 Mana) Surrounding Sands: When you fall below 10% HP, a sand wall emerges within Reach. The wall is a Line with Scale 1 in a direction of your choosing. While behind this sand wall you gain Cover 3.

**Keyword:** Concentration, Harmless

**Restoration**  
**Keyword:** Concentration, Harmless  
**Difficulty:** 3  
**Optimal Range:** Reach  
**Base Cast:**  
Restore vital essence to a creature so it may live again. The spell revives a creature to life, for the creature to remain alive the creature must heal all injuries and the caster must maintain concentration for the next 24 hours or the creature will die again.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (4 Mana) Complete the spell: The spell no longer requires concentration.
- (4 Mana) Request more time: The spell last for an additional 24 hours.

**IP Spend:**

- 3 IP: The spell also restores one injury.

**Call for Aid**  
**Keyword:** Lasting, Oathsworn  
**Difficulty:** 2  
**Base Cast:**  
You ask your Oathsworn God for aid and summon minions of type Normal from their domain to aid you. The spell lasts until the end of the scene. Minions and their associated God:

- The Obscured: Eldritch Beings. Acts in combat on it's own unless commanded.
- The Fiery: Flame Elementals. Acts in combat on it's own unless commanded.
- The Ferocious: Spirit Beasts. Acts in combat on it's own unless commanded.
- The Vengeful: Vicious Beings. Acts in combat on it's own unless commanded.
- The Quasar: Virtuous beings. Acts in combat on it's own unless commanded.
- The Soulless: Undead beings. Acts in combat on it's own unless commanded.
- The Luminescent: Crystal Elementals. Acts in combat on it's own unless commanded.
- The Hearth of the Sand: Sand Elementals. Acts in combat on it's own unless commanded.
- The Erudite: Scholars to Aid you with Lore tests. Will roll assistance on your tests, GM controls these.
- The Artisan: Henchmen to Aid you with Craft tests. Will roll assistance on your tests, GM controls these.
- The Sentinel: Shield Bearers to Aid you with Parry tests. Will roll assistance on your tests, GM controls these.

**Variety Cast:**

- (5 Mana) Competence: The summoned creature is Mighty.
- (7 Mana) Of Legends: The summoned creature is Legendary.
- (2 Mana) In Abundance: Summon additional Normal creature.

**Avatar of the Gods**  
**Keywords:** Consentration, Oathsworn  
**Difficulty:** 3  
**Base Cast:**  
Become the embodiment of your oathsworn God. This Powerful spell increases you Max Health equal to you current level, it increases damage of all your weapon attacks by 4 CD. Difficulty of Religion spells reduced by 1. Immunity to Necrotic and Increase to Physical and Magical DR by 3. The spell lasts for 3 rounds, but increase by 2 for each enemy killed.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (3 Mana) Lasting empowerment: The spell lasts for 3 additional rounds.
- (3 Mana) True Faith: No longer requires concentration.

**Condemn**  
**Keywords:** Consentration  
**Difficulty:** Struggle  
**Base Cast:**  
You condemn another character actions using you CHA + Arcana against their RES + Discipline. If the target fails, the target is overwhelmed with guilt and can no longer use the Move or Sprint Actions. The target makes a RES + Discipline test Difficulty 1 at the end of their turn. If successful the caster must make a consentration test, if failed all consentration spells end including this one.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (2 Mana) No Conviction: Choose a skill and increase the difficulty of all tests the target makes with that skill.
- (4 Mana) Flawed: Choose a attribute and increase the difficulty of all tests the target makes with that attribute.
- (3 Mana) Mass Condemnation: Choose a zone. All characters within that zone must succeed the struggle or be affected by the spell.

**IP Spend:**

- 2 IP: Increase the Difficulty to break free from the spell.

**Cleansing Flame**  
**Keyword:** Concentration  
**Difficulty:** Target Defense + 1  
**Optimal Range:** M  
**Base Cast:**  
You engulf a target in a cleansing flame that persist while concentrating. At the start of each of your turns, the target takes 4 CD damage with the Vicious 1 Effect and Intense Quality.  
**Variety Cast:**

- (4 Mana) Make them suffer: Add Vicious 1 to the damage.
- (4 Mana) Purify: While concentrating, if the target gains 4 Injuries it dies.
- (4 Mana) Jumping Flame: If this spell kills it's target, move it to another within Close Range for free.
- (6 Mana) Burn the Soul: This damage ignores all DT.

Glimmershards
 
You throw out 3 shards, they deal 1 CD each and attach to the target. When a target with a shard is attacked, the shard explodes for an additional 1CD.
 
- 1 Mana: Throw additional shards.
- 3 Mana: Shards gain Spread on explosion damage.
- 2 Mana: Increase impact damage.
- 2 Mana: Increase explotion damage.
