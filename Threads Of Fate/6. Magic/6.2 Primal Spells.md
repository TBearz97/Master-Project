**Imbue Elements**
**Tags:** Concentration, Elemental, Weapon
**Difficulty:** 1
**Base Cast:**
Draw elemental force into a weapon. The weapon gains an elemental expression while the spell lasts.

- Earth: The weapon strikes with crushing force and can Stun.
- Air: The weapon becomes lighter and quicker in motion.
- Water: The weapon gains freezing force and can apply Freeze.
- Fire: The weapon gains Fire and can apply Burning.

**Variety Cast:**
- (2 Mana) Additional Element: Imbue the weapon with an additional element.
- (X + 1 Mana) Conjured Vessel: Conjure a weapon to carry the chosen element.
- (4 Mana) Lasting Bond: The elemental bond lasts until the end of the scene.

**Conjure a Storm**
**Tags:** Concentration, Weather
**Difficulty:** 1
**Optimal Range:** C
**Base Cast:**
Conjure a storm that obscures vision across a small area.

- (2 Mana) Blizzard: The area becomes difficult terrain.
- (3 Mana) Vortex: Creatures have difficulty escaping the storm's pull.
- (4 Mana) Lightning: Lightning strikes creatures within the storm and can Stun.
- (4 Mana) Widen Storm: Increase the scale of the storm.

**Nature's Balm**
**Tags:** Brief, Healing, Poison
**Difficulty:** Varies
**Optimal Range:** Reach
**Base Cast:**
Conjure and apply a magical balm. The balm may heal, soothe, or poison depending on how it is prepared.

- Healing: Restore Health over time.
- Toxins: Deliver a poisonous attack through touch.

**Variety Cast:**
- (4 Mana) Curing: Remove one poison, disease, or similar harmful condition.
- (3 Mana) Sickening: The poisonous version also becomes Infectious.
- (2 Mana) Potent: Increase the damage or healing.
- (1 Mana) Additional Portion: Create an extra dose.
