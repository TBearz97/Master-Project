Tabletop roleplaying games are an exciting and immersive way to share your own personal story in a fantasy world. Together with your gamemaster and fellow players, you will explore and complete quests while building your own unique character through the actions and decisions you make. When you want to achieve something in the game, such as a task or challenge, you will need to attempt a skill test to determine your success or failure. The game rules help resolve the outcome of the test, and the gamemaster then continues to narrate the story based on your actions, and how non-player characters and creatures react. Play is freeform, allowing each player to act freely in no particular order, and the passage of time is based on what makes sense for the actions of the group. When engaging in combat, play is structured into rounds and turns, with each player taking their turn during each round. Through this collaborative and immersive gameplay, you will have the opportunity to create and explore your own unique story in a rich and exciting world.

# **Book Structure**
The book is divided up into multiple chapters and is structured in a way that teaches you segments in a structured order should you wish to read this entire book front to back. However this book serves better as a dictionary to look up specific questions and jump to and from the sections you are the most curious about.

Starting with Core Rules. This section teaches you how to play and most of the book builds on top of these rules, this section is quite important for each player to have a fair understanding over in order to ease play. However the app should still enable you to play without this knowledge, but this section does help in order of figuring out what is happening beneath the hood of the application.
