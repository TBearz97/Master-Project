In Threads of Fate, two important resources are Action Points and Mana. These are used for different things, and it is important to understand what each one does.

# **Action Points**
Action Points, usually shortened to AP, show how much a character can do in a short amount of time.

If a character wants to move, attack, defend, use an item, or take some other important action, that usually costs AP. A character with more AP can do more before their turn or opportunity ends.

Action Points do not measure power. They measure activity. In simple terms, AP tells you how much a character can do.

# **Mana**
Mana is a resource used to fuel powerful abilities. It is often used for spells, but Mana is not only for magic in the narrow sense. It represents stored power that can be used to strengthen special actions and effects.

Characters gain Mana over time from Resolve. They can also gain more Mana by taking Focus actions. Some perks and abilities may change how Mana is gained or used.

# **The Difference**
Action Points and Mana are not used for the same purpose.

Action Points decide how much you can do.

Mana helps determine how much power you can put into certain actions.

For example, a character may spend AP to act now, or spend AP on Focus in order to build Mana for something stronger later.

The exact rules for spending AP and Mana are explained later in the book.
