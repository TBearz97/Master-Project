# **Upkeep**  
After time spent on the road it can be really relaxing to enjoy a couple days in a city or a village. You can enjoy talking to the locals, browse their shops to replenish lost equipment or perhaps just enjoy a drink or two in the local tavern. During downtime the party chooses how long they are planning to stay, if they stay for a full week they can choose a downtime activity that will sum up their stay. However food and sleep for the duration is not free, this is referred to as Upkeep. Instead of doing the logistics of buying a room, food and drink for each day, it's all summed up as a Upkeep value based on which society you want to stay in. For each day you also heal a flat amount to represent you drinking and eating during the day. However a full week of downtime will always set your health to full. Note that some villages and cities does not have all societies.

### **Squalid Details**  
You'll have shelter from weather, access to dirty water and uncooked foods. Each day living in these conditions comes with the risk of a disease, make a END + Resistance test with Difficulty 2. A failed test contracts a disease.
 
### **Poor Details**  
You have access to simple food and clean water as well as a room, however you might need to share the room with others from time to time with limited privacy. Each day make a PER + Society test with Difficulty 1. A failed test results in 1d20 Gold being stolen.
 
### **Modest Details**  
You get a private room, cooked meals and clean water. You are safe from disease and crime during your stay.
 
### **Comfortable Details**  
You get a private room with a proper bed, cooked meals and clean water. At the end of this stay you get the Well-Rested Bonus.
 
### **Wealthy Details**  
You have access to a small house with a comfortable bed, cooked meals and a selection of drinks. At the end of this stay you get the Well-Rested Bonus as well as you can choose a effect from drinks, this lasts until the next time you rest, but ignore alcoholic effects for this purpose.
 
### **Aristocratic Details**  
You have access to a Mansion, cooked meals and a selection of drinks. At the end of this stay you get the Well-Rested Bonus as well as you can choose a effect from drinks, this lasts until the next time you rest, but ignore alcoholic effects for this purpose. During your stay you also get a butler, the butler reduces all Society tests by 1.
# **Downtime Activities**
 
When you are staying in a village or city for a week your GM will ask you to choose a downtime activity for that period of time. This may require one of more Skill Tests and those will determine the outcome at the end of the week. The available downtime activities will vary at the GM's discretion based on the town, city or village you find yourself in.
 
## **Crafting:**  
If you're looking to create something unique or improve an existing item, you can use the craft skill to spend a week working on your project. Whether you're fashioning a new weapon or adding extra defensive capabilities to your armor, your skill and creativity will determine the final result. The more rare and complex the item you're trying to craft, the more challenging the task will be. For more information see the crafting section.
 
## **Training:**  
If you want to improve your skills or learn something new, you can spend a week training with a particular tool, weapon, or spell. This may require you to seek out a master craftsman, warrior, or mage to learn from, or it may simply involve studying on your own. The difficulty of learning a new skill or item will depend on its rarity and complexity.
 
## **Performing:**  
If you have a talent for entertaining others, you can spend a week performing for the people. 
 
## **Carousing:**  
If you're looking to network and build a reputation, you can spend a week socializing with other people.

## **Browsing:**  
You can always ask the DM to go shopping for specific common items like a different weapon, some food or general supplies. However if you are looking for something out of the normal, like magic weapons, rare potions or high quality armor, then you would need to spend your downtime browsing different stores and tracking down your specific item. Further details can be found in the [[5.1 Equipment#**Finding and Purchasing Items**|Finding and Purchasing Items]] section.
 
## **Re-Adjusting:**  
If you need to refocus your efforts or change your approach, you can spend a week re-adjusting your skills and perks. By spending gold, you can swap out a tag skill for another or exchange one perk for a different one. Any perks that you no longer meet the requirements for can be swapped out for free.
## **Research**: 
Delve into tomes or visit sages to uncover lore about a person, place, artifact, beast or something else.

## **Gathering Resources**: 
Hunt, forage, or mine for materials like herbs, metals, or food from the local environment.

## **Local Labor**: 
Take up a temporary job by helping locals with various tasks.

## **Recovering:**
Rest to heal injuries, recover from curses, or gain lost hit points.

## **Worship/Prayer:** 
Visit a temple or shrine to seek blessings or guidance.
