Death Spitter  
Large undead monster that have an unknown origin, largely presumed to merely be myth. The creature has a large mouth that can unhinge it's jaws to either spit at a enemy or leave necrotic sludge beneath itself. The sludge will seep into the earth, should it come in contact with a dead creature, that creature will raise from the dead in the following days. These Death Spitters occasionally scour battlefields or graveyards amassing a undead army to protect itself. After raising an army they will find a hive to live in while their undead gather food and protect it.
