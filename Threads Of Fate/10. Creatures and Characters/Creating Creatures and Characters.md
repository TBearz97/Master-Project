Minion creatures are slightly weaker than Normal creatures.

- 38 Attribute points
- Skill points equal to 9 + INT
- Health is equal to END + RES
 
Normal creatures follow same rules as players.

- 40 Attribute points
- Skill points is equal to 9 + INT + Level
- Health is equal to END + RES + Level
 
Mighty creatures are slightly stronger than players.

- 42 Attribute points
- Skill points is equal to 9 + INT + Level
- Health equal to 2 * END + RES + Level
 
Legendary creatures are equally strong as Mighty, but have one attributes to be at a legendary level.

- 42 Attribute points + 1 Legendary Attribute
- Skill points is equal to 9 + INT + Level
- Health equal to 2 * END + RES + Level
 
Legendary Attributes gain a special perk and is always 2 points higher than the maximum.￼Legendary Attributes:

- Strength - Melee Damage is increased by 3 CD. Carry Weight doubled.
- Perception - Gains Exploit as a Free Action. Automatically has 2 successes for spotting stealth creatures.￼
- Endurance - This character regains 3CD health at the start of every turn.
- Charisma - Can swap initiatives of subordinates once per round.
- Intelligence - Reduce difficulty for all tests by 1.
- Agility - This character has a second initiative equal to Agility score.
- Resolve - Cannot be charmed or lose concentration.
      

Beast attacks can be bite, talons, claws, horns, etc. Base damage for all these are 3 CD Piercing 1, and increases by 1 CD every 5th level.  
Perks for Beast:  
Nimble - Defense increased by 1.  
Fur - 1 DR to all.  
Large - +2 DR to magical and physical.  
Pack Animal - 1d20 re-roll when attacking the same target as an ally.  
Wings - Can fly and cannot be hit on it's turn.  
Fast - Attacks benefit from Light Quality and Dual-Wielding  
Strong - +2 CD damage.  
Venomous - Attacks gain Persistent (Poison) Effect.  
Charging - If the beast takes a Minor Action Move and moves into an adjacent zone to attack a target. The attack gains Vicious 1 and Knockdown.
