Melee weapons are one of the most common ways of fighting and protecting yourself. Anyone can pick something up and start swinging with it.

Improvised weapons are anything not listed below that you still choose to swing with. A character does not start with proficiency with improvised weapons. Improvised weapons deal 1D4 physical damage per 5 kilo it weighs. Larger objects might do more damage but require an Athletics test to be lifted. Improvised weapons might also break after any attack, even without complication, at the GM's discretion.

# **Melee Attacks**
Each melee weapon has its own collection of seven attacks. These are divided into one Basic attack, two Proficiency attacks, two Expertise attacks, and two Mastery attacks. Individual attacks may be shared between different weapons, but each weapon should end up with its own final package.

## **Basic**
- **Jab** - A quick close strike meant to create an opening.
- **Lash** - A simple strike that keeps the target at a distance.
- **Slash** - A simple cutting strike meant to pressure the target.
- **Reap** - A broad harvesting swing that cuts through space in front of you.
- **Strike** - A direct and reliable hit with the weapon's weight or length.
- **Smash** - A forceful basic blow meant to stagger through impact.
- **Chop** - A heavy edged attack that commits to a decisive cut.
- **Swing** - A straightforward hammering attack with solid momentum.
- **Stab** - A direct forward thrust intended to keep the enemy at reach.
- **Quick Slash** - A fast cut that relies on speed over commitment.
- **Thrust** - A precise forward lunge aimed at a narrow opening.

## **Proficiency**
- **Clinch** - You crash in close and control the enemy's space with your body.
- **Pounce** - You lunge aggressively to overwhelm the target before they can reset.
- **Snap** - A sharp cracking strike meant to disrupt timing and posture.
- **Feint** - You bait a reaction before redirecting into the real attack.
- **Hook Cut** - A curving cut that pulls around a guard or catches a retreat.
- **Hook** - A hooking motion used to drag, redirect, or unbalance the target.
- **Clobber** - A rough battering blow designed to knock the target off balance.
- **Body Blow** - A committed strike to the middle meant to steal breath and stance.
- **Drive** - You step through the target with force to push them backward.
- **Draw Cut** - A cut delivered as the blade moves free in one fluid motion.
- **Riposte** - A sharp answer delivered the moment an opening appears.
- **Hook Swing** - A chained or spiked swing that comes around from an awkward angle.

## **Expertise**
- **Elbow** - A brutal short-range strike delivered from tight positioning.
- **Rend** - A savage tearing attack meant to open flesh and leave a lasting wound.
- **Wrap** - You curl the attack around the target to interfere with their position.
- **Gash** - A deeper deliberate wound intended to keep bleeding pressure on the target.
- **Wing Clip** - A strike meant to ruin footing and cut off clean movement.
- **Kneecapper** - A low crushing attack aimed at breaking stability and movement.
- **Overhead Swing** - A high committed blow brought down with punishing force.
- **Cleave** - A wide committed swing intended to threaten more than one foe.
- **Skewer** - A long driving thrust meant to pin the target in place.
- **Sweep** - A low or broad attack that takes away footing and space.
- **Thousand Cuts** - A relentless series of shallow strikes that build pressure quickly.
- **Twist** - A precise turn of the weapon after contact to deepen the harm.
- **Piercer** - A hard puncturing attack that forces through protection with intent.
- **Crescent Arc** - A flowing curved swing meant to catch multiple lines at once.

## **Mastery**
- **Palm Strike** - A perfectly placed burst of force that stops the target in place.
- **Choke Hold** - A dominating hold that shuts down the enemy's options at close range.
- **Breaker** - A punishing finishing technique that turns leverage into raw harm.
- **Maul** - A ferocious chain of slashing force that overwhelms the target completely.
- **Drag Line** - A controlling whip technique that tears through posture and positioning.
- **Serpent's Kiss** - A precise whip finish that punishes the smallest mistake.
- **Kidney Strike** - A cruel close blow aimed at dropping the target's strength instantly.
- **Open Vein** - A perfected cut placed exactly where a wound will matter most.
- **Heart Seeker** - A thrust or lunge aimed with lethal certainty at a vital opening.
- **Silence Cut** - A quiet finishing attack meant to end the fight before it can turn.
- **Harvest Stroke** - A seasoned reaping blow that carries through with deadly control.
- **Final Reaping** - The full murderous expression of the reaper's reach and curve.
- **Vaulting Blow** - A mobile staff strike that turns movement into impact.
- **Spinning Guard** - A mastering flourish that attacks while controlling surrounding space.
- **Skull Crack** - A crushing club strike meant to finish with blunt certainty.
- **Groundbreaker** - A devastating impact that shakes bodies and footing alike.
- **Bone Crusher** - A maul strike delivered with enough force to ruin whatever it lands on.
- **Tomb Hammer** - A full-bodied maul swing meant to end resistance outright.
- **Split Guard** - A disciplined axe strike that opens a target through broken defense.
- **Executioner's Fall** - A committed descending cut that ends hesitation and mercy.
- **World Splitter** - A great axe blow delivered with maximum force and commitment.
- **Bell Ringer** - A head-rattling hammer strike that leaves the target reeling.
- **Armor Knell** - A hammer technique meant to punish even heavily protected foes.
- **Crater Maker** - A hammer blow that lands with brutal finality.
- **Flatten** - A warhammer strike that turns weight and leverage into domination.
- **King's Judgement** - A finishing hammer blow delivered with complete authority.
- **Cross Cut** - A disciplined follow-up that turns one opening into another.
- **Half-Sword** - A close controlling technique that uses the blade with precise leverage.
- **Pommel Crush** - A sudden crushing strike from the weapon's hilt or butt.
- **Duelist's End** - A refined finishing attack that closes the fight cleanly.
- **Guard Break** - A mastery strike designed to shatter structure and force an opening.
- **Falling Star** - A great sword blow that carries the full arc of the weapon through the target.
- **Giant's Sentence** - A heavy finishing cut that leaves no room for recovery.
- **Pinning Rush** - A forward-driving spear technique that keeps the enemy under pressure.
- **Boar Hunter** - A spear finish meant to stop even a charging foe dead.
- **Brace** - A planted pike technique that turns the enemy's momentum against them.
- **Phalanx Push** - A formation-minded drive that claims ground through reach and force.
- **Hedge of Iron** - A battlefield technique that locks down space with repeated reach.
- **Flash Step** - A sudden movement cut delivered before the target can reset.
- **Moon Slice** - A smooth cutting finish built on perfect timing and control.
- **Rising Edge** - An ascending blade path that turns defense into offense.
- **Falling Blossom** - A graceful but lethal descending cut that completes the exchange.
- **Heaven Split** - A mastery slash delivered with overwhelming reach and precision.
- **Red Horizon** - A huge finishing cut that sweeps through everything before it.
- **Chain Snap** - A violent flail motion that batters through timing and guard.
- **Helmshaker** - A crushing impact aimed to leave the target stunned and broken in posture.
- **Ruin Chain** - A relentless chained assault that keeps the enemy from recovering space.
- **Black Comet** - A spiked finishing strike that lands with terrifying inevitability.
- **Whirling Step** - A curving moving attack that changes angle mid-assault.
- **Sand Reaper** - A cutting sequence meant to exhaust and carve the target down.
- **Twin Moon Cut** - A flowing finishing pattern that strikes in a seamless pair.
- **Final Salute** - A perfected rapier finish delivered with confidence and precision.
- **Harvest Moon** - A broad polearm mastery sweep that claims the battlefield around you.
- **Headsman's Reach** - A halberd finish delivered from just outside a normal guard.

# **Melee Weapons Complications**
The following are suggestions of possible complications that might occur while wielding a melee weapon. Roll a d4 for one of the complications.

- **(1) Fumble:** You fumble as you strike the target, reducing the impact. The attack does not benefit from the damage multiplier.
- **(2) Overextended:** Your attack leaves you unable to effectively defend yourself; reduce all your defenses by 2 until the start of your next turn.
- **(3) Wear and Tear:** The weapon has seen better days, and no longer functions as well as it once did. Reduce the weapon's reliability. If it was already unreliable and must be reduced again, the weapon breaks beyond repair.
- **(4) Stuck:** Your weapon has gotten lodged in something - maybe the terrain around you, maybe the enemy. You'll either need to release your grip on the weapon, or retrieve it using the Interact Action.
