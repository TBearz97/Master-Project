Melee Weapons are one of the most common ways of fighting and protecting your self. Any one can pick something up and start swinging with it. However proficiency with the weapon means you truly know how to use it to it's full effect.
 
Should you lack proficiency with the weapon you are currently wielding you suffer the following effects:

- You do not benefit from the damage multiplier
- You do not benefit from the weapons Qualities or Effects
- You cannot use special attacks with the weapon
 
Improvised weapons are anything not listed below that you still choose to swing with. A character does not start with proficiency with improvised weapons. Improvised weapons deal 1D4 physical damage per 5 kilo it weights. Larger objects might do more damage but requires an Athletics test to be lifted. Improvised weapons might also break after any attack, even without complication, at the GMs discretion.

# **Melee Weapons and Their Special Attacks**
Combat emphasizes versatility, tactical depth, and the unique qualities of each weapon type. This approach ensures that every weapon and attack serves a distinct role, rewarding players who master their equipment and combat strategies.

## **Special Attacks**
Special attacks allow melee weapons to transcend simple damage-dealing. Each weapon type has unique options that reflect its strengths and playstyle. These attacks balance risk and reward by modifying speed and adding effects, but requires targeting specific hit locations.

## **Precision Blades**
The Precision Blades consists of small, lightweight weapons designed for quick, precise strikes. These blades are favored by rogues, assassins, and anyone requiring stealth and agility in combat. Their compact size makes them ideal for close-quarters engagements or throwing when needed. While lacking in raw power, their speed and accuracy ensure they remain a deadly option in skilled hands.

- **Knife**: A short blade used for cutting or thrusting.
- **Dagger**: A small, pointed weapon used for stabbing.
- **Stiletto**: A slender dagger with a pointed blade, designed for thrusting.
- **Rapier**: A slender, sharply pointed sword designed for thrusting.

# **Melee Weapons Complications**  
The following are suggestions of possible complications that might occur while wielding a melee weapon. Roll a d4 for one of the complications

- **(1) Fumble:** You fumble as you strike the target reducing the impact. The attack does not benefit from the damage multiplier
- **(2) Overextended:** Your attack left you unable to effectively defend yourself; reduce all your defenses by 2 until the start of your next turn.
- **(3) Wear and Tear:** The weapon has seen better days, and no longer functions as well as it once did. Reduce the weapon’s reliability. If it was unreliable and you were forced to reduce it, the weapon breaks beyond repair.
- **(4) Stuck:** Your weapon has gotten lodged in something— maybe the terrain around you, maybe the enemy. You’ll either need to release your grip on the weapon (which prevents you from using it, obviously), or retrieve it using the Interact Action.
