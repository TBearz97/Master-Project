**Beverages**
Beverages typically restore some measure of a character's Health, or have some other benefit, with a few doing both. Unlike food, drinks can be consumed during combat. You can only drink a single beverage each scene. Most beverage effects last until the end of the current scene unless noted otherwise.

Many of the beverages listed in this section are Alcoholic. All alcoholic drinks have the same effect: until the end of the scene, you may re-roll 1d20 on Might and Charisma tests, but Intelligence tests become harder. In addition, alcoholic drinks are addictive.

**Potions and Herbs**
Potions and Herbs can be administered to yourself or someone else within Reach, as long as the recipient is willing. A consumable's effects are Instant, Brief, or Lasting. The benefits of Potions and Herbs do not stack: if two Potions and Herbs provide the same benefit, whichever was taken most recently takes precedence. Many Potions and Herbs are addictive.
