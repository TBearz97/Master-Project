Clothing provides little protection by itself, but it can still matter in everyday life and harsh environments. Clothing may be worn under armor.

Outfits are complete worn sets that provide both protection and a thematic bonus. Outfits cannot be combined with armor at all. Like all equipped items, both clothing and outfits contribute to Equip Load.

Not wearing any clothing or outfit counts as being exposed to cold weather if temperatures are below 68 F / 20 C and counts as being exposed to hot weather if above. See more information on exposure in the Survival section.

**Alchemist's Attire**
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on any test to Craft at an Alchemy Workstation.

**Blacksmith's Outfit**
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on any test to Craft at an Armor Smith Workstation or Weapon Smith Workstation.

**Bandit's Outfit**
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on any Stealth test.

**Commoner's Clothing**
Common clothing offers basic everyday protection with no special bonus beyond its modest DT.

**Desert Clothing**
While wearing this clothing, you can increase your Survival rank for determining exposure to hot weather by 1.5.

**Fur Clothing**
While wearing this clothing, you can increase your Survival rank for determining exposure to cold weather by 1.5.

**Mercenary's Outfit**
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on attacks made with hired or issued battlefield equipment, at the GM's discretion.

**Nature's Gown**
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on Arcana tests that use Endurance.

**Noble's Attire**
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on Speech and Society tests.

**Performer's Outfit**
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on any test to Perform during downtime and on Speech tests made to entertain, distract, or command attention.

**Pirate's Attire**
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on any Navigation test made at sea.

**Priest's Garments**
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on Arcana tests that use Charisma.

**Scribe's Robe**
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on any test to Craft at a Scribe Workstation.

**Ranger's Outfit**
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on any Survival test.

**Tribal Outfit**
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on melee-related skill tests.

**Wizard's Robe**
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on Arcana tests that use Intelligence.
