Clothing provides little protection by itself but can combined with armor worn over.
 
Outfits grant bonuses, at the cost of being unable to combine them with armor. You can wear armor on top of Outfits, but loose the bonus effect and DT does not stack.
 
Not wearing any clothing or outfit counts as being exposed to cold weather if temperatures are below 68° F/20° C and counts as being exposed to hot weather if above. See more info on exposure in the Survival Section.

**Alchemist's Attire**  
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on any test to Craft at an Alchemy Workstation.
 
**Blacksmith's Outfit**  
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on any test to Craft at an Armor Smith Workstation or Weapon Smith Workstation.
 
**Bandit's Outfit**  
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on any Stealth test.
 
**Desert Clothing**  
While wearing this outfit, you can increase your Survival rank for determining exposure to hot weather by 1.5.
 
**Fur Clothing**  
While wearing this outfit, you can increase your Survival rank for determining exposure to cold weather by 1.5.
 
**Mercenary's Outfit**  
While wearing this outfit, you gain 2 DT to physical and magical damage on all locations covered.
 
**Nature's Gown**  
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on Arcana tests that uses END.
 
**Nobel's Attire**  
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on Speech and Society tests.
 
**Performer's Outfit**  
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on any test to Perform during downtime and Inspire during combat.
 
**Pirate's Attire**  
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on any Navigation test made on sea.
 
**Priest's Garments**  
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on Arcana tests that uses CHA.
 
**Scribe's Robe**  
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on any test to Craft at a Scribe Workstation.
 
**Ranger's Clothing**  
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on any Survival test.
 
**Tribal Outfit**  
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on any Melee Weapons test.
 
**Wanderer's Leather Clothing**  
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on any Navigation test.
 
**Wizard's Robe**  
While wearing this outfit, you can re-roll 1d20 on Arcana tests that uses INT.
