Ranged weapons are ideal for characters who want to strike from a distance and avoid getting trapped in the middle of the fray. Different ranged weapons support different fighting styles, from quick close-range shooting to long-range precision.

Ammunition is not tracked in Threads of Fate. Bows, crossbows, and firearms are assumed to have enough arrows, bolts, powder, and shot for normal play unless the GM introduces a special circumstance.

# **Ranged Attacks**
Each ranged weapon has its own collection of seven attacks. These are divided into one Basic attack, two Proficiency attacks, two Expertise attacks, and two Mastery attacks. Individual attacks may be shared between different weapons, but each weapon should end up with its own final package.

## **Basic**
- **Blast Shot** - Fire a broad close-range blast meant to overwhelm anything directly ahead.
- **Quick Shot** - Fire a fast and direct shot before the enemy can settle.
- **Fire** - Release a steady aimed shot from a long firearm.
- **Loose Arrow** - Release an arrow in one clean practiced motion.
- **Bolt Shot** - Fire a bolt with simple force and reliability.

## **Proficiency**
- **Load Shot** - Reload and ready the firearm for another attack.
- **Point-Blank Shot** - Fire at extremely close range with total commitment.
- **Aimed Shot** - Take the extra moment needed to place the attack exactly where you want it.
- **Center Mass** - Aim for the largest opening and drive the shot through with force.
- **Quick Nock** - Draw and set the next arrow with practiced speed.
- **Crippling Shot** - Place the shot to hinder movement and break the target's stride.
- **Heavy Draw** - Pull the shot with full strength to deliver more force on release.
- **Load Bolt** - Reload and ready the crossbow for another attack.
- **Point-Blank Bolt** - Drive a bolt into the target from almost no distance at all.
- **Hidden Release** - Fire from concealment or surprise before the enemy properly reads the threat.

## **Expertise**
- **Scatter Burst** - Spread the blast wide to catch multiple targets in a dangerous space.
- **Fan the Hammer** - Fire in rapid succession before the enemy can react cleanly.
- **Suppressing Shot** - Force the enemy to keep their head down and give up space.
- **Snap Volley** - Send several arrows skyward to threaten a wider area.
- **Split Shot** - Fire with the intent of threatening more than one target or line at once.
- **Storm Volley** - Fill an area with arrows and force everyone within it to react.
- **Pinning Bolt** - Place a heavy bolt so it fixes the target in place or ruins their movement.
- **Deadweight** - Fire a punishing bolt that drags the target's posture and momentum down.
- **Breach Bolt** - Fire a reinforced shot meant to punch through protection and cover.

## **Mastery**
- **Breach Shot** - A devastating blast intended to tear through protection at close range.
- **Thunder Mouth** - A concussive close-range discharge that leaves survivors reeling.
- **Street Sweeper** - The full brutal expression of a scatter gun in cramped fighting.
- **Deadeye** - A perfectly measured pistol shot delivered with total confidence.
- **Execution Line** - A finishing firearm shot meant to end resistance immediately.
- **Marksman's Patience** - Hold the shot until the perfect opening appears and then exploit it.
- **Last Light** - A musket shot delivered with final decisive force across long distance.
- **Running Shot** - Loose an arrow cleanly while still moving and repositioning.
- **Rain of Thorns** - Layer an area with arrows so movement through it becomes dangerous.
- **Hawk's Wake** - A sharp finishing bow shot released at the exact moment the target breaks.
- **Hunter's Mark** - A practiced hunting shot that fixes the target and pressures every later attack.
- **Quiet Ending** - A precise lethal arrow delivered without flourish or wasted motion.
- **Impaling Flight** - A powerful arrow that drives deep and leaves the target in serious trouble.
- **Field Breaker** - A war bow shot meant to disrupt the enemy line through raw force.
- **Eagle Eye** - A long shot placed with exceptional clarity and control.
- **Battlefield Harvest** - A dominating long bow technique that punishes anyone left exposed.
- **Quiet Finish** - A silent close shot that ends the exchange before it widens.
- **Vein Hunter** - A wickedly placed crossbow shot aimed at the most vulnerable point available.
- **Wall Nailer** - A heavy bolt that hammers the target into place with terrifying force.
- **Siege Line** - A brutally committed arbalest shot made to break what others cannot.
- **Dragon Pin** - The fullest expression of the arbalest: a monstrous bolt placed to stop even the hardest target.

# **Ranged Weapons Complications**
The following are suggestions of possible complications that might occur while wielding a ranged weapon. Roll a d4 for one of the complications.

- **(1) Fumbled:** You mishandle the weapon while readying your next shot, making the next attack with that weapon slower than normal.
- **(2) Overextended:** Your attack leaves you unable to effectively defend yourself; reduce your defenses by 2 until the start of your next turn.
- **(3) Wear and Tear:** The weapon has seen better days, and no longer functions as well as it once did. Reduce the weapon's reliability. If it was already unreliable and must be reduced again, the weapon breaks beyond repair.
- **(4) Misfire or Snap:** Something goes wrong with the weapon's firing mechanism or string. You must use an Interact Action on the weapon before you can use it again.
