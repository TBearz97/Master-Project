# **Armor**
Armor protects specific locations on the body and increases your Damage Threshold against different kinds of harm. Heavier and more protective equipment contributes more weight to your Equip Load, while lighter equipment allows you to remain more evasive.

# **Equip Load**
Equip Load represents the burden of everything your character currently has equipped and ready for use. This includes armor, clothing, outfits, weapons, and quick items. Equip Load is determined by Might.

You cannot exceed your maximum Equip Load. If equipping an item would push you past that limit, then you cannot equip that item until something else is removed.

For now, Equip Load is divided into three bands:

- **Light Load:** Up to one third of your maximum Equip Load. Gain +3 Deflection.
- **Medium Load:** More than one third, up to two thirds of your maximum Equip Load. Gain +2 Deflection.
- **Heavy Load:** More than two thirds, up to your maximum Equip Load. Gain +1 Deflection.

Anything beyond your maximum Equip Load cannot be equipped at all.
